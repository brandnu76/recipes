Delicious, Creamy Mashed Potatoes

Rich make-ahead mashed potatoes with butter, cream cheese, and half-and-half, seasoned with Lawry's and warmed in the oven under pats of butter.

Adapted from The Pioneer Woman

Ingredients Steps
10 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 10 servings

Prep: 20 minutes

Cook: 55 minutes

Total: 1 hour 15 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 5 lb russet or Yukon Gold potatoes
  • 3/4 cup (1 1/2 sticks) butter, plus a few pats for the top
  • 1 (8-oz) package cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 to 3/4 cup half-and-half
  • 1/2 to 1 tsp Lawry's Seasoned Salt
  • 1/2 to 1 tsp black pepper

Instructions

  1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into evenly sized pieces. Put them in a large pot of simmering water, bring to a boil, and cook 30 minutes, until a fork slides in with no resistance and they almost fall apart.
  2. Drain the potatoes well, return them to the dry pot over low heat, and mash them, letting the steam escape, about 2 minutes. Heat the oven to 350°F.
  3. Turn off the heat and add the butter, cream cheese, and about 1/2 cup of the half-and-half. Mash until smooth, then season with the seasoned salt and black pepper, adding more half-and-half if you like them looser.
  4. Spread the potatoes in a medium baking dish, dot the top with pats of butter, and bake 15 minutes, until the butter melts and the potatoes are hot.

Notes: Make them up to 2 days ahead and refrigerate before the oven step; bake about 30 minutes from cold.
