Christmas Cranberry Butter

A deep-red, slow-cooked cranberry butter with orange, brown sugar, and warm holiday spices. Thick and glossy like classic apple butter — perfect on toast, biscuits, or stirred into oatmeal. Makes a beautiful holiday gift.

Ingredients Steps
16 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: about 2 cups (roughly 16 servings)

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 4 hours (mostly unattended)

Total: about 4 hours 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 24 ounces (about 6 cups) fresh or frozen cranberries, rinsed
  • 1 cup fresh orange juice
  • Zest of 2 oranges
  • 1 cup packed light brown sugar
  • ½ cup granulated sugar
  • 1 cinnamon stick (or 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon)
  • ½ teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • ¼ teaspoon ground cloves
  • ¼ teaspoon ground ginger
  • Pinch of fine sea salt
  • 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract (added at the end)

Instructions

  1. Add the cranberries, orange juice, orange zest, brown sugar, granulated sugar, cinnamon stick, nutmeg, cloves, ginger, and salt to a slow cooker. Stir to combine.
  2. Cover and cook on high for 2 hours, or until the cranberries have burst and the mixture is soft and juicy. (Stovetop option: simmer covered in a heavy pot over medium-low heat for 30–40 minutes, stirring occasionally.)
  3. Remove the cinnamon stick. Puree the mixture until completely smooth using an immersion blender, food processor, or blender (work carefully with hot liquid). For an extra-smooth texture, press through a fine-mesh sieve.
  4. Return the puree to the slow cooker. Cook uncovered on high for another 1½ to 2 hours, stirring every 20–30 minutes, until thick, glossy, and reduced to a spreadable butter consistency (it should mound on a spoon and leave a trail when stirred). It will thicken further as it cools.
  5. Stir in the vanilla. Taste and adjust sweetness or spices if desired. Transfer to clean jars, cool completely, then refrigerate. Keeps in the fridge for up to 3 weeks or freeze for longer storage.

Notes: Serve on toast, biscuits, pancakes, or cheese boards. It also makes excellent holiday gifts. For a deeper flavor, cook an extra hour. The butter is naturally high-acid from the cranberries and orange.
