Strawberry Strudel Skillet Cookie

Cinnamon skillet cookie with strawberry-lemon filling and almond streusel.

IngredientsSteps
8 servings

Ingredients

  • Butter, sugars, eggs, flour, cinnamon, strawberries, lemon, cornstarch, almonds, and powdered sugar

Instructions

  1. Cook the strawberry filling.
  2. Make the cookie dough and layer it with filling and streusel.
  3. Bake until golden, cool, glaze, and serve.
