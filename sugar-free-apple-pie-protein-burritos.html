Sugar-Free Apple Pie Protein Burritos

By Brandon Carroll

Crisp baked apple pie burritos with sugar-free canned filling, cream cheese, high-protein tortillas, and a cold vanilla Greek yogurt protein cream. About 24 g protein each.

  • Serves 4 burritos
  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Total 30 min
  • Category Dessert

Ingredients

  • 1 (21-oz) can sugar-free (no sugar added) apple pie filling
  • 4 large high-protein tortillas (8 g+ protein each)
  • 1 cup plain 2% Greek yogurt
  • 1 1/2 scoops (about 45 g) vanilla whey protein powder
  • 2 oz Neufchatel or light cream cheese, softened
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon, divided
  • Pinch of nutmeg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Pinch of kosher salt
  • 1 tbsp butter, melted
  • 1 tbsp granulated monk fruit or allulose (optional)

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400\u00b0F and line a sheet pan with parchment.
  2. Whisk the Greek yogurt, whey, vanilla, and a pinch of salt until smooth and frosting-thick. Chill.
  3. Pour off about 2 tbsp of thin syrup from the apple pie filling, chop large slices into 1/2-inch pieces, and stir in 1 tsp cinnamon, the nutmeg, and lemon juice.
  4. Mix the remaining 1/2 tsp cinnamon with the sweetener.
  5. Warm the tortillas 15 seconds so they fold without cracking.
  6. Spread 1/2 oz cream cheese down the center of each tortilla and top with about 1/2 cup apples in a 2-inch strip.
  7. Fold in the sides, roll tight, and set seam-side down on the pan.
  8. Brush with melted butter and sprinkle with the cinnamon mix.
  9. Bake 12\u201315 minutes, until deep golden and crisp and the filling bubbles at the ends.
  10. Rest 5 minutes, then top each with a spoonful of cold protein cream.

Notes

  • Why the whey stays cold: baked whey turns chalky and rubbery, so the protein powder goes in the cold cream, not the filling.
  • Cast iron: medium heat with 1/2 tbsp butter, seam-side down first, 2\u20133 minutes per side.
  • Pellet grill: 400\u00b0F on the same parchment-lined sheet pan, 12\u201315 minutes. Apple or cherry pellets.
  • Air fryer: 375\u00b0F for 7\u20139 minutes, seam-side down.
  • Leftovers: wrapped burritos (no cream) keep 3 days refrigerated. Reheat at 375\u00b0F for 6\u20138 minutes, not the microwave. Store the cream separately.
  • Estimated macros per burrito: about 310 calories, 24 g protein, 37 g carbs, 10 g fat. Estimates vary by tortilla and protein powder brand.
