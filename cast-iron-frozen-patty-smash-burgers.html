Cast-Iron Frozen Patty Smash Burgers with Caramelized Onions

Frozen burger patties seared straight from the freezer in cast iron, pressed for a crust, topped with melty cheese, golden onions, and a quick special sauce.

Ingredients Steps
4 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 4 burgers

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 25 minutes

Total: 35 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 4 frozen hamburger patties (1/4 to 1/3 lb each)
  • Kosher salt and freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 large yellow onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 to 2 Tbsp butter or beef tallow
  • 4 slices American, cheddar, or pepper jack cheese
  • 4 soft hamburger buns
  • Pickles, shredded lettuce, tomato, or bacon, optional
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 1 Tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tsp yellow mustard
  • 1 tsp pickle relish or finely chopped pickles
  • Pinch of garlic powder and smoked paprika
  • Splash of hot sauce, optional

Instructions

  1. Stir the mayonnaise, ketchup, yellow mustard, pickle relish, garlic powder, smoked paprika, and hot sauce together for the special sauce. Set aside.
  2. Heat a cast-iron skillet over medium heat. Add the butter and onion with a pinch of salt and cook, stirring now and then, until soft and deep golden, about 12 minutes. Push the onion to one side or move it to a plate.
  3. Turn the heat to medium-high and add a thin film of tallow if the pan looks dry. Lay the frozen patties in the skillet (no thawing) and season the tops with salt and pepper. Leave them alone for 4 minutes to build a crust.
  4. Flip, season the second side, and press each patty firmly with a spatula. Cook 4 minutes more.
  5. Top each patty with cheese and let it melt, about 1 minute, until the burgers reach 160°F. While the cheese melts, toast the buns cut side down in the skillet or under the broiler.
  6. Spread special sauce on both halves of each bun, add a patty, a pile of onions, and any toppings, and serve right away.

Notes: Pellet grill option: heat the grill to 375°F, season both sides of the frozen patties, and cook directly on the grates with the lid closed, flipping every few minutes, until 160°F inside (usually 15 to 20 minutes total). Add the cheese in the last minute and cook the onions in a small cast-iron pan on the grill alongside.
