This product was produced at a residential property that is exempt from state inspection. This product may contain allergens.

MADE IN A COTTAGE FOOD OPERATION THAT IS NOT SUBJECT TO STATE FOOD SAFETY INSPECTIONS

Lowcountry Table Bakery · Ludowici, Georgia (Long County)

Everything is baked in a home kitchen that also handles common allergens: wheat, milk, eggs, soy, peanuts, tree nuts (including pecans, walnuts and almonds), sesame, fish and shellfish. Cross-contact is possible.

No decorated or birthday cakes. Everything on the menu is shelf-stable and needs no refrigeration. Pickup only in Ludowici; sales within Georgia only.
