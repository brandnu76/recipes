Sourdough Croissants

A laminated, lightly enriched dough leavened with active sourdough starter, with a little yeast for insurance. Flaky, shattering layers and a honeycomb crumb with a gentle tang.

Ingredients Steps
12 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 12 croissants

Prep: 60 minutes

Cook: 20 minutes

Total: 48 hours (includes overnight rests and proofing)

Difficulty: Weekend

Ingredients

  • 500 g (about 4 cups) bread flour
  • 100 g active sourdough starter, fed and at peak
  • 250 g (1 cup) whole milk
  • 55 g (1/4 cup) sugar
  • 30 g (2 tbsp) unsalted butter, softened, for the dough
  • 10 g (1 3/4 tsp) fine salt
  • 3 g (1 tsp) instant yeast, optional
  • 280 g (2 1/2 sticks) cold European-style unsalted butter, for laminating
  • 1 egg plus 1 tbsp cream, for egg wash

Instructions

  1. Day 1: Mix the flour, sugar, salt, yeast, starter, milk, and softened butter 5-6 minutes until smooth but not fully elastic. Shape into a flat rectangle, wrap, and rise at room temperature 2-3 hours until about 50% bigger. Refrigerate overnight.
  2. Day 1: Pound and roll the cold laminating butter between parchment into a 7-inch square. Refrigerate.
  3. Day 2: With both cold and the butter pliable (55-60°F), roll the dough to a 10-inch square. Set the butter on it diagonally, fold the corners over, and pinch the seams.
  4. Roll to an 8x24-inch strip and do a letter fold. Wrap and chill 30-45 minutes.
  5. Repeat the roll and letter fold twice more, chilling between folds.
  6. Roll to an 8x30-inch strip, about 1/4 inch thick. Cut tall triangles with a 4-inch base. Notch the base and roll up gently, tip underneath.
  7. Proof covered on parchment-lined pans at 75-78°F for 6-10 hours, until jiggly with visible layers. Keep below 80°F.
  8. Heat the oven to 400°F. Brush with egg wash and bake 18-22 minutes until deep mahogany. Cool 15 minutes.

Notes: Temperature is everything; keep the butter and dough cold and similar in firmness. Suggested schedule: mix Friday, laminate and shape Saturday, bake Sunday morning. Best the same day. Freeze unbaked shaped croissants, then proof overnight before baking. Revive day-olds 5 minutes at 350°F.
