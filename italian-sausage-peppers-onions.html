Italian Sausage, Peppers, and Onions

Browned sweet Italian sausage simmered with buttery red and yellow onions, bell peppers, garlic, herbs, and a splash of white wine. Pile it into buns.

Adapted from Allrecipes

Ingredients Steps
6 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 6 servings

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 35 minutes

Total: 45 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 6 (4-oz) links sweet Italian sausage
  • 2 Tbsp butter
  • 1 yellow onion, sliced
  • 1/2 red onion, sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 large red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 green bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/4 cup white wine
  • 6 hot dog buns or hoagie rolls

Instructions

  1. Brown the sausage on all sides in a large skillet over medium heat, about 10 minutes. Move it to a board and slice.
  2. Melt the butter in the skillet. Add the yellow onion, red onion, and garlic and cook 3 minutes.
  3. Add the red bell pepper and green bell pepper, season with the basil and oregano, and stir in the white wine. Cook, stirring, until the peppers and onions are tender, about 7 minutes.
  4. Return the sausage to the skillet, lower the heat, cover, and simmer 15 minutes, until the sausage is cooked through.
  5. Spoon into the buns and serve.

Notes: AnyList showed this doubled; amounts are back at 1x. AnyList had 1 red onion at the 2x view, which is 1/2 at 1x. Chicken broth stands in fine for the wine.
