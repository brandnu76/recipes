Hominy Grill-Style Shrimp and Grits

The Charleston classic: floured shrimp sautéed in bacon drippings with mushrooms, garlic, lemon, and green onions, spooned over creamy cheddar grits.

Adapted from MyRecipes (Hominy Grill, Charleston)

Ingredients Steps
6 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 6 servings

Prep: 30 minutes

Cook: 20 minutes

Total: 50 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 lb unpeeled medium raw shrimp (31/40 count)
  • 2 Tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 5 slices bacon, chopped
  • 1 (8-oz) package sliced fresh mushrooms
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/3 cup fresh lemon juice
  • 1/2 cup thinly sliced green onions
  • 2 tsp hot sauce
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 batch Creamy Cheddar Cheese Grits, for serving

Instructions

  1. Peel the shrimp and devein if you like. Toss them with the flour until lightly coated and shake off the extra.
  2. Cook the bacon in a medium skillet over medium-high heat until crisp, about 8 minutes. Move it to paper towels and keep the drippings in the skillet.
  3. Sauté the mushrooms in the hot drippings for 4 minutes, until they start to release their liquid.
  4. Add the shrimp and sauté 3 minutes, just until pink. Add the garlic and cook 1 minute without browning it.
  5. Stir in the lemon juice, green onions, hot sauce, and salt. Spoon over the grits right away and sprinkle with the bacon.

Notes: Make the Creamy Cheddar Cheese Grits first and keep them warm; the shrimp cooks fast.
