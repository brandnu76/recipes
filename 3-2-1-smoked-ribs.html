3-2-1 Smoked Ribs with Apple Spritz

Mustard-and-rub ribs smoked low at 225°F with an apple juice spritz, wrapped with barbecue sauce and apple juice, then finished under a honey-brown sugar glaze.

Adapted from gimmesomegrilling.com

Ingredients Steps
8 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 8 servings

Prep: 20 minutes

Cook: 6 hours

Total: 8 hours 20 minutes

Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 2 racks pork ribs
  • 4 Tbsp yellow mustard
  • 1 batch of your favorite dry rib rub
  • 1 1/2 cups bbq sauce, divided (1/2 cup for the wrap, 1 cup for the glaze)
  • 1 cup apple juice, divided
  • 1/2 cup water, for the spritz
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp honey
  • 1 1/2 Tbsp brown sugar

Instructions

  1. Slide a dinner knife under the membrane on the bone side of each rack, lift until it tears, then grab the edge with a paper towel and pull it off. Get it all; it keeps the rub out and turns leathery.
  2. Spread the yellow mustard over both racks, then coat completely with the dry rub. Wrap tightly in plastic wrap and refrigerate at least 2 hours.
  3. Set the smoker or pellet grill to 225°F (apple wood is a great match). Mix 1/2 cup of the apple juice with the water in a spray bottle.
  4. Lay the ribs bone side down on the grate and smoke for 3 hours, spritzing with the apple juice mix every half hour.
  5. Pull the ribs and leave the smoker running at 225°F. Set each rack on a large sheet of foil. Stir 1/2 cup bbq sauce with the remaining 1/2 cup apple juice, pour it over the racks, and seal the foil tight. Return to the smoker for 2 hours.
  6. Unwrap the ribs. Stir the remaining 1 cup bbq sauce with the honey and brown sugar, brush it over the racks, and smoke unwrapped for 1 hour more.
  7. Brush with a little more glaze, slice between the bones, and serve.

Notes: For the best flavor, let the rubbed ribs sit in the fridge overnight instead of the 2-hour minimum. Spare ribs or St. Louis cut take the full 3-2-1; baby backs usually want closer to 2-2-1. See also the 3-2-1 Baby Back Ribs with the brown sugar and honey wrap.
