Keto Slow Cooker Braised Oxtails

Oxtails slow-cooked in a buttery beef broth with soy, fish sauce, tomato paste, and thyme until falling off the bone, with the juices blended into gravy.

Adapted from ruled.me

Ingredients Steps
3 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 3 servings

Prep: 20 minutes

Cook: 7 hours 5 minutes

Total: 7 hours 25 minutes

Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 2 lb bone-in oxtails
  • 2 cups beef broth
  • 1/3 cup butter
  • 2 Tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 Tbsp fish sauce
  • 3 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp minced garlic
  • 1/2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1/2 tsp guar gum

Instructions

  1. Warm the beef broth in a saucepan on the stove and whisk in the soy sauce, fish sauce, tomato paste, and butter until the butter melts, about 5 minutes.
  2. Put the oxtails in the slow cooker, pour the broth over them, and season with the onion powder, garlic, ginger, thyme, salt, and pepper.
  3. Cover and cook on low for 7 hours, until very tender.
  4. Lift out the oxtails and set them aside (on paper towels if you want them dry).
  5. Sprinkle the guar gum into the juices and blend with an immersion blender until thickened.
  6. Serve the oxtails with the gravy spooned over, alongside cauliflower mash or another rich side.

Notes: Check at 6 hours; meatier oxtails may need the full 7 or longer. Guar gum keeps it keto; a cornstarch slurry works if carbs don't matter.
