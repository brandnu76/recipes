Lemon Skillet Cookie

Giant butter skillet cookie with crisp golden edges, a soft lemony center, zest in the dough, and a sharp lemon glaze.

Ingredients Steps

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 8 servings

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 22-28 minutes

Total: about 45 minutes

Ingredients

  • 3/4 cup unsalted butter, melted and cooled 5 minutes (170 g), plus more for the pan
  • 1/2 cup packed light brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 large egg yolk
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 2 tablespoons packed lemon zest (from about 2 lemons)
  • 1 3/4 cups all-purpose flour (220 g)
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 3/4 cup white chocolate chips (optional)
  • 3/4 cup powdered sugar
  • 2 to 3 tablespoons lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 350 F. Soft-butter a 10-inch cast-iron skillet.
  2. Zest both lemons (about 2 tablespoons packed), then juice them (you need 2 to 3 tablespoons for the glaze).
  3. Whisk melted butter with brown sugar and granulated sugar until glossy. Beat in the egg, egg yolk, vanilla, and all the lemon zest.
  4. In another bowl whisk flour, baking soda, baking powder, and salt. Stir dry ingredients into wet just until no dry flour remains. Fold in white chocolate chips if using. Dough will be thick and sticky.
  5. Press dough evenly into the skillet, edge to edge. Do not tamp rock-hard.
  6. Bake 22 to 28 minutes, until the rim is deep golden, the top is set, and the center still looks slightly soft. A thermometer in the cookie (not the pan) should read about 195 to 200 F.
  7. Cool in the skillet 10 minutes. Whisk powdered sugar with 2 to 3 tablespoons lemon juice until thick but pourable; drizzle over the warm cookie. Serve warm, scooped or sliced.
