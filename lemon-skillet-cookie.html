Lemon Skillet Cookie

Giant butter skillet cookie with crisp golden edges, a soft lemon center, and lemon glaze.

IngredientsSteps
8 servings

Ingredients

  • Butter, brown sugar, granulated sugar, egg, lemon zest, flour, baking soda, baking powder, salt, powdered sugar, and lemon juice

Instructions

  1. Whisk the cooled butter, sugars, egg, vanilla, and lemon zest.
  2. Stir in the dry ingredients and press the dough into a buttered skillet.
  3. Bake until the edges are golden and the center is soft; cool and drizzle with lemon glaze.
