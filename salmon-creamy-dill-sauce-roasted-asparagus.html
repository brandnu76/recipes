Salmon with Creamy Dill Sauce and Roasted Asparagus

Salmon fillets and asparagus roasted together on one pan, served with a quick sour cream, dill, lemon, and Dijon sauce.

Ingredients Steps
4 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 4 servings

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 15 minutes

Total: 25 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets (about 6 oz each)
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil, plus a little more for the asparagus
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 lb asparagus, trimmed
  • 1/2 cup sour cream (or Greek yogurt)
  • 1 Tbsp fresh dill, chopped
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 clove garlic, minced

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F and line a sheet pan with parchment.
  2. Set the salmon on the pan, drizzle with the olive oil, and season with salt and pepper. Arrange the asparagus beside it, drizzle with a little more oil, and season.
  3. Roast 12 minutes, until the salmon flakes easily and the asparagus is crisp-tender.
  4. While it roasts, stir together the sour cream, dill, lemon juice, Dijon mustard, garlic, and a pinch of salt and pepper.
  5. Plate each fillet with a big spoonful of dill sauce and the asparagus alongside.

Notes: Thicker fillets may need 15 minutes. Skin-on salmon (fattier belly pieces especially) stays the most moist.
