High-Protein Strawberry Shortcake

A no-cook strawberry shortcake bowl: nonfat Greek yogurt, sugar-free vanilla pudding mix, stevia, crushed freeze-dried strawberries, and vanilla wafers, folded with chopped fresh strawberries. Two servings.

Ingredients Steps
2 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 2 servings

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 0 minutes

Total: 10 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 3 tablespoons sugar-free vanilla pudding mix
  • 1 tablespoon sweetener (stevia)
  • 1/2 cup freeze-dried strawberries
  • Splash of vanilla extract
  • 1/4 cup fresh strawberries, chopped
  • 4 vanilla wafers

Instructions

  1. Mix and crush together the Greek yogurt, sugar-free vanilla pudding mix, sweetener, freeze-dried strawberries, vanilla extract, and vanilla wafers until the wafers and freeze-dried berries are broken up and the base is smooth.
  2. Fold in the chopped fresh strawberries. Divide into 2 servings and eat right away.

Notes

Pulled from Josh New (@jnewtrition) on TikTok. He credits the original to Mia Carson. Amounts, order, yield, and macros are the ones in the caption.

The pudding mix goes in dry. Do not cook it. Crush the freeze-dried strawberries and the vanilla wafers into the yogurt so they soften as you mix.

Creator numbers are for the entire recipe, not one bowl: 420 calories and 50 g protein. Split in half, that is about 210 calories and 25 g protein per serving. No carb or fat number was given. Brands move those numbers.
