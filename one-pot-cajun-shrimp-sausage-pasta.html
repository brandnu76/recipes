One-Pot Cajun Shrimp and Sausage Pasta

Linguine tossed with Cajun butter shrimp, smoky kielbasa, onion, and green pepper in a quick creamy Cajun sauce, all in one pot.

Adapted from keyingredient.com

Ingredients Steps
4 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 4 servings

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 25 minutes

Total: 35 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 lb linguine
  • 3 Tbsp butter, divided
  • 2 1/2 Tbsp Cajun seasoning, divided
  • 1 lb large shrimp, peeled and deveined
  • 1/2 cup diced yellow onion
  • 1/2 cup diced green pepper
  • 1 lb kielbasa sausage, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1/2 cup chicken stock
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • Chopped parsley, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Cook the linguine in a large pot of boiling water until al dente, about 10 minutes. Drain and set aside.
  2. In the same pot over medium-high heat, melt half the butter and stir in 3/4 Tbsp of the Cajun seasoning. Add the shrimp and cook until pink, about 3 minutes. Move the shrimp to a plate.
  3. Melt the rest of the butter in the pot, add the onion and green pepper, and cook until the onion is translucent, about 4 minutes.
  4. Add the kielbasa and cook 1 minute, then stir in the garlic, the rest of the Cajun seasoning, the red pepper flakes, and the thyme.
  5. Pour in the chicken stock and heavy cream, stir well, and simmer 3 minutes, until it starts to thicken.
  6. Return the shrimp and linguine to the pot and toss until coated. Sprinkle with parsley and serve.

Notes: AnyList showed this doubled; amounts are back at 1x. Two teaspoons of red pepper flakes is hot; start with 1 if you want it milder. Andouille is a great swap for the kielbasa.
