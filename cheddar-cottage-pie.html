Cottage Pie with Cheddar Mashed Potato Top

A rich ground beef, carrot, and pea filling simmered with tomato paste, Worcestershire, and beef bone broth, under a buttery cheddar mashed potato crust baked golden in a 9x13. A double batch, so Friday is leftovers.

Ingredients Steps
10 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 10 servings

Prep: 25 minutes

Cook: 50 minutes

Total: 1 hour 15 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 3 lb ground beef, 80/20
  • 4 lb russet potatoes, peeled and cut into 2-inch chunks
  • 8 Tbsp salted butter
  • 3/4 cup whole milk
  • 8 oz sharp cheddar cheese, shredded
  • 2 large onions, diced
  • 3 carrots, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 3 Tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups beef bone broth
  • 3 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tsp dried thyme
  • 2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 cups frozen peas

Instructions

  1. Cover the russet potatoes with cold salted water in a large heavy pot and boil until fork-tender, 20 minutes. Heat the oven to 400°F while they cook.
  2. Meanwhile, brown the ground beef in a deep HexClad pan over medium-high heat, 8 minutes. Add the onions and carrots and cook 5 minutes, then the garlic and tomato paste for 2 minutes.
  3. Sprinkle in the flour and stir 1 minute. Add the beef bone broth, Worcestershire sauce, thyme, salt, and pepper and simmer until thick, 8 minutes. Stir in the frozen peas and spread the filling in a 9x13 baking dish.
  4. Drain the potatoes and mash them with the butter, milk, and three-quarters of the cheddar, 3 minutes. Season with salt.
  5. Spread the mash over the filling, rough it up with a fork, and sprinkle on the rest of the cheddar.
  6. Bake 25 minutes, until bubbling at the edges and golden on top. Rest 5 minutes before serving.

Notes: This is a double batch for Friday's leftovers: refrigerate the rest covered and reheat at 350°F for 25 minutes, or by the square in the microwave. A deep 9x13 is full to the brim, so set it on a sheet pan to catch drips. For a richer filling, finely chop 4 oz chicken livers and brown them with the beef; no one will notice them.
