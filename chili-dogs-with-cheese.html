Chili Cheese Dogs

Beef franks in soft buns, buried under hot dog chili, shredded cheese, and green onions. Game-day easy.

Adapted from hormel.com

Ingredients Steps
8 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 8 chili dogs

Prep: 5 minutes

Cook: 10 minutes

Total: 15 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 8 beef franks
  • 8 hot dog buns, split
  • 1 (15-oz) can hot dog chili
  • Shredded cheese, for topping
  • Sliced green onions, optional

Instructions

  1. Cook the franks according to the package directions, about 6 minutes. Warm the hot dog chili in a small saucepan at the same time.
  2. Tuck the franks into the buns, spoon the hot chili over them, and top with cheese and green onions.

Notes: AnyList showed this doubled; amounts are back at 1x. Toast the buns on the grill or in a skillet for sturdier dogs. Pair with Simple, Perfect Chili instead of canned for a homemade version.
