Thai Red Curry Chicken over Jasmine Rice

Boneless chicken thighs simmered in full-fat coconut milk and red curry paste with bell peppers and green beans, finished with lime and basil over jasmine rice. A double batch, so Friday is leftovers.

Ingredients Steps
8 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 8 servings

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 25 minutes

Total: 40 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 3 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into bite-size pieces
  • 3 cups jasmine rice
  • 4 1/2 cups water
  • 2 Tbsp coconut oil
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • 2 red bell peppers, sliced
  • 12 oz green beans, trimmed and halved
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 Tbsp fresh ginger, grated
  • 1/2 cup red curry paste
  • 3 (13.5 oz) cans full-fat coconut milk
  • 1 cup chicken bone broth
  • 3 Tbsp fish sauce
  • 2 Tbsp brown sugar
  • 2 limes, juiced
  • 1 cup fresh basil leaves

Instructions

  1. Rinse the jasmine rice and combine it with the water in a large saucepan. Bring to a boil, cover, and simmer on low 15 minutes, then rest off the heat, covered, 10 minutes.
  2. While the rice cooks, heat the coconut oil in a large heavy pot over medium-high heat. Cook the onion 3 minutes, then add the garlic, ginger, and red curry paste and fry, stirring, 2 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Add the chicken thighs and stir to coat, 3 minutes. Pour in the coconut milk and chicken bone broth and bring to a simmer.
  4. Simmer 10 minutes, then add the red bell peppers and green beans and simmer 5 minutes more, until the chicken is cooked through and the vegetables are crisp-tender.
  5. Stir in the fish sauce, brown sugar, and lime juice, then the basil. Taste and add more fish sauce or lime. Serve over the jasmine rice.

Notes: This is a double batch: save half for Friday's leftovers. The curry reheats beautifully on the stove; make fresh jasmine rice Friday since day-old rice dries out. Curry paste brands vary in heat, so start with 1/3 cup if yours is spicy.
