Baked Mac and Cheese with Smoked Sausage

Creamy Velveeta, cheddar, and mozzarella macaroni loaded with sliced beef smoked sausage, topped with buttery panko, and baked until bubbly.

Adapted from justapinch.com

Ingredients Steps
10 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 10 servings

Prep: 30 minutes

Cook: 45 minutes

Total: 1 hour 15 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 lb macaroni
  • 12 oz Velveeta cheese, cubed
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1 package (about 14 oz) beef smoked sausage, sliced
  • 1 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 stick butter, melted, plus a few pats for the top
  • 1/2 cup panko bread crumbs

Instructions

  1. Cook the macaroni in a large pot of salted water until just tender, about 8 minutes. Rinse, drain, and toss with the melted butter in a large casserole dish. Heat the oven to 350°F.
  2. Put the Velveeta, cheddar, and mozzarella in a saucepan with the heavy cream, red pepper flakes, garlic powder, and salt and pepper. Melt over medium-low heat, stirring often, about 8 minutes.
  3. Pour the cheese sauce over the macaroni, add the smoked sausage, and stir to combine.
  4. Top with the panko and a few pats of butter.
  5. Bake 45 minutes, until bubbly and golden.

Notes: A fatty smoked sausage or kielbasa works as well as beef. Brown the sausage coins in a skillet first for extra flavor.
