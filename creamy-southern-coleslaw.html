Classic Creamy Southern Coleslaw

A quick, creamy, slightly sweet slaw from a bag of coleslaw mix, mayo, sour cream, cider vinegar, and lemon.

Ingredients Steps
6 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 6 servings

Prep: 10 minutes

Total: 40 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 (14-oz) bag coleslaw mix
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/4 cup sour cream
  • 1 Tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Whisk the mayonnaise, sour cream, apple cider vinegar, sugar, lemon juice, salt, and black pepper together in a large bowl.
  2. Add the coleslaw mix and toss until evenly coated.
  3. Chill 30 minutes so the cabbage softens a little, then toss again and serve.

Notes: It's good right away too, but the short chill lets the flavors come together. Pile it on pulled pork or barbecue sandwiches.
