Fried Cabbage with Kielbasa

Browned kielbasa and buttery fried cabbage with onion, garlic, a splash of red wine vinegar, and paprika. Low carb and on the table in 30 minutes.

Adapted from peaceloveandlowcarb.com

Ingredients Steps
6 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 6 servings

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 20 minutes

Total: 30 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 6 Tbsp butter, divided (or olive oil or ghee)
  • 1 cup diced onion (about 4 oz)
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 Tbsp red wine vinegar
  • 14 oz kielbasa, thinly sliced on a bias
  • 1 large head green cabbage, cored and sliced
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • Sea salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1/4 cup Italian flat-leaf parsley, roughly chopped
  • 1 tsp crushed red pepper flakes, optional

Instructions

  1. Melt 3 Tbsp of the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and garlic and cook until the onion is translucent, about 4 minutes.
  2. Stir in the red wine vinegar.
  3. Add the kielbasa and cook until lightly browned, about 5 minutes.
  4. Add the rest of the butter, the cabbage, paprika, salt, and pepper and toss until the cabbage is coated.
  5. Cook, stirring now and then, until the cabbage is wilted and browned in spots, about 10 minutes.
  6. Top with the parsley and red pepper flakes and serve.

Notes: AnyList showed this doubled; amounts are back at 1x. Use a big pan; the cabbage cooks down a lot.
