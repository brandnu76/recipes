Syracuse Salt Potatoes

Upstate New York's cookout classic: small new potatoes boiled in heavily salted water until creamy inside with a salty crust, drowned in melted butter.

Adapted from Allrecipes

Ingredients Steps
8 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 8 servings

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 20 minutes

Total: 30 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 4 lb new potatoes
  • 1 1/2 cups fine salt
  • 8 Tbsp butter, melted

Instructions

  1. Wash the potatoes. Fill a large pot with water and stir in the fine salt until no more will dissolve and some settles on the bottom.
  2. Add the potatoes, bring to a boil, then lower to a simmer and cook 15 minutes, until tender but still firm. Drain and cover to keep hot.
  3. Melt the butter in a small pan or the microwave and pour it over the hot potatoes to serve.

Notes: As the potatoes dry, a thin white salt crust forms; that's the point. They pair well with the low-country boil and grilled meats.
