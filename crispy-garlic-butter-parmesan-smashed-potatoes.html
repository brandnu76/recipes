Crispy Garlic Butter Parmesan Smashed Potatoes

Baby Yukon Golds boiled tender, smashed flat on a sheet pan, and roasted hot in garlic butter until the edges shatter, then finished under a blanket of Parmesan.

Adapted from cafedelites.com

Ingredients Steps
8 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 8 servings

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 50 minutes

Total: 1 hour

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

Potatoes

  • 2 lb baby Yukon Gold potatoes, about 1 1/2 inches across
  • 2 Tbsp kosher salt, for the boiling water
  • 1 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil

Garlic butter

  • 3 Tbsp butter, melted
  • 4 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 Tbsp chopped fresh parsley, plus more to serve
  • 1/2 tsp flaky or kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper

Finish

  • 1/2 cup finely grated Parmesan

Instructions

  1. Put the potatoes in a large pot, cover with cold water by an inch, and add the kosher salt. Bring to a boil over high heat, about 10 minutes.
  2. Boil the potatoes 15 to 20 minutes, until a knife slides through with no resistance. Drain and let them steam dry in the colander for 5 minutes; drier skins crisp better.
  3. Meanwhile, heat the oven to 425°F with a rack in the upper third. Brush a rimmed sheet pan with the olive oil.
  4. Set the potatoes on the sheet pan with room between them. Press each one gently with the bottom of a glass or a potato masher until it splits and flattens to about 1/2 inch, still in one piece.
  5. Stir the melted butter, garlic, parsley, salt, and black pepper together and spoon it over the potatoes, letting it run into the cracks.
  6. Roast 25 minutes, until the edges are deep golden and crisp. No need to flip.
  7. Scatter the Parmesan over the potatoes and roast 5 minutes more, until it melts and lacy bits brown at the edges. Finish with parsley and serve hot.

Notes: Same oven as the sprouts: both roast at 425°F, so they share the oven on steak night while the ribeye sits on the pellet grill. Pellet grill option: run the grill at 425°F and roast the pan there for the same time; you get a light kiss of smoke. Swap in half Parmesan, half Pecorino for more bite. Keeping the skins on keeps the fiber and potassium. Adapted from the smashed potatoes at cafedelites.com, reworked for a hot sheet pan roast instead of the broiler.
