Southern Fried Cabbage with Bacon

Chopped cabbage fried slow with bacon, onion, bell pepper, and butter until tender and sweet, seasoned simply with seasoned salt and pepper.

Adapted from Allrecipes

Ingredients Steps
6 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 6 servings

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 35 minutes

Total: 45 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 6 slices bacon, chopped
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 1 green bell pepper, chopped
  • 1 head cabbage, chopped
  • 1/4 cup butter
  • Seasoned salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Cook the bacon, onion, and bell pepper together in a large skillet over medium heat until the bacon is just starting to brown, about 10 minutes.
  2. Add the cabbage and butter and cook, stirring often, until the cabbage is tender and translucent, about 25 minutes.
  3. Season with the seasoned salt and black pepper and serve.

Notes: The saved recipe's steps used bacon, onion, and bell pepper but left them out of the ingredient list; the amounts here are added to match. A deep skillet or wide pot gives the cabbage room as it cooks down.
