Smoked Spatchcock Chicken with Carolina Gold Sauce and Smashed Red Potatoes

A whole flattened chicken smoked on the pellet grill then crisped hot, mopped with tangy South Carolina mustard sauce, with crispy smashed red potatoes roasted right alongside.

Ingredients Steps
4 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 4 servings

Prep: 25 minutes

Cook: 2 hours 5 minutes

Total: 2 hours 30 minutes

Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 1 whole chicken (4 to 5 lb)
  • 2 Tbsp barbecue rub
  • 1 Tbsp kosher salt
  • 2 lb small red potatoes
  • 4 Tbsp salted butter, melted
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 Tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • 3/4 cup yellow mustard
  • 1/4 cup honey
  • 1/4 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1/3 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp hot sauce
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper

Instructions

  1. Cut out the chicken's backbone with kitchen shears, flip it, and press firmly on the breastbone to flatten it. Pat dry and season all over with the salt and barbecue rub, 10 minutes.
  2. Preheat the pellet grill to 275°F, about 15 minutes. While it heats, boil the red potatoes in salted water in a large heavy pot until fork-tender, 20 minutes, then drain.
  3. Smoke the chicken skin side up at 275°F for 60 minutes.
  4. Meanwhile, simmer the yellow mustard, honey, brown sugar, apple cider vinegar, Worcestershire sauce, hot sauce, and black pepper in a small saucepan over medium-low heat, 5 minutes, for the Carolina gold sauce.
  5. Smash each potato flat on an oiled sheet pan, brush with the melted butter and olive oil, and sprinkle with the garlic powder and salt.
  6. Raise the grill to 400°F. Put the potato pan on the grill next to the chicken and cook 35 minutes, until the chicken breast reads 160°F and the potatoes are crisp. Brush the chicken with some of the sauce for the last 10 minutes.
  7. Rest the chicken 10 minutes, then carve. Sprinkle the potatoes with the parsley and serve with the rest of the sauce.

Notes: For the crispiest skin, salt the chicken and leave it uncovered in the fridge overnight. Carolina gold sauce keeps 2 weeks in the fridge. Boil the potatoes earlier in the day if you like; they smash even better cold.
