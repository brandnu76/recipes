Blueberry Crumble

Blueberries cooked down with lemon juice and zest on the stove, then baked under a buttery brown sugar oat streusel in an 8-inch square pan.

Ingredients Steps
8 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 8 servings

Prep: 20 minutes

Cook: 45 minutes

Total: 1 hr 35 min (includes 30 minutes cooling)

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

Blueberry filling

  • 5 cups blueberries, fresh or frozen
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 3 Tbsp cornstarch
  • 2 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • 1/8 tsp kosher salt

Oat streusel

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 3/4 cup old-fashioned rolled oats
  • 2/3 cup packed light brown sugar
  • 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 cup (1 stick) cold unsalted butter, cubed

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F with a rack in the middle. Butter an 8-inch square baking pan or set out an 8-inch square foil pan on a sheet pan, about 5 minutes.
  2. Make the streusel: in a bowl, whisk the flour, oats, brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt. Work in the cold butter with your fingertips until it forms clumps the size of peas, about 5 minutes. Refrigerate it while you cook the filling.
  3. In a large saucepan, stir together the blueberries, granulated sugar, cornstarch, lemon juice, lemon zest, and salt, about 5 minutes.
  4. Cook over medium heat, stirring often, until the juices bubble and turn thick and glossy, 5 minutes. Take the pan off the heat.
  5. Spread the filling in the prepared pan. Scatter the streusel evenly over the top, pressing a few clumps together for big crumbs, about 5 minutes.
  6. Bake at 375°F until the filling bubbles at the edges and the streusel is deep golden, about 40 minutes. If the top browns too fast, lay foil loosely over it near the end.
  7. Cool on a rack at least 30 minutes so the filling sets before serving.

Notes: Frozen blueberries work well: use 5 cups straight from the freezer, without thawing, and cook 3 minutes longer until the juices thicken. Bakery sale: cool the crumble completely, about 2 hours, before covering it, then keep it covered at room temperature and sell it within 1 to 2 days of baking. Sell it plain, with no ice cream, whipped cream, or cream topping; at home, a scoop of vanilla ice cream on a warm serving is fine. Contains wheat and milk.
