Red Wine Braised Short Ribs

Meaty bone-in beef short ribs seared hard, then oven-braised for hours in red wine and beef bone broth until they fall off the bone, served over creamy mashed potatoes in their glossy sauce.

Ingredients Steps
6 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 6 servings

Prep: 40 minutes

Cook: 3 hours 20 minutes

Total: 4 hours

Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 5 lb bone-in beef short ribs (English cut)
  • 1 Tbsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 Tbsp beef tallow or olive oil
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 stalks celery, diced
  • 6 cloves garlic, smashed
  • 3 Tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 Tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 1 bottle (750 ml) dry red wine, such as Cabernet
  • 2 cups beef bone broth
  • 4 sprigs fresh thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 Tbsp salted butter
  • 2 Tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 325°F. Season the short ribs all over with the salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the beef tallow in a large heavy oven-safe pot with a lid over medium-high heat. Sear the short ribs in batches until deeply browned on all sides, 15 minutes total. Move them to a plate.
  3. Pour off all but 2 Tbsp fat. Cook the onion, carrots, and celery over medium heat 6 minutes, then add the garlic and tomato paste for 2 minutes. Stir in the flour for 1 minute.
  4. Pour in the red wine, scrape up the browned bits, and boil until reduced by about a third, 8 minutes. Add the beef bone broth, thyme, and bay leaves.
  5. Return the short ribs bone side up, cover, and braise in the oven 3 hours, until fork-tender.
  6. Move the short ribs to a platter. Skim the fat off the sauce, simmer it on the stove until glossy, 10 minutes, then whisk in the butter. Spoon the sauce over the ribs, sprinkle with parsley, and serve over mashed potatoes.

Notes: For a 6:30 dinner, start searing at about 2:00 so the pot goes in the oven around 2:30. Serve over Creamy Mashed Potatoes, made while the sauce reduces. Even better made a day ahead: refrigerate in the pot overnight, lift off the hardened fat, and rewarm at 325°F for 45 minutes. Save the skimmed fat for roasting potatoes.
