Classic Hawaiian Loco Moco

Juicy seasoned beef patties over jasmine rice, smothered in mushroom gravy and topped with a sunny-side-up egg.

Adapted from tastingtable.com

Ingredients Steps
4 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 4 servings

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 25 minutes

Total: 40 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1/2 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 egg, for the patties
  • 1/4 cup panko bread crumbs
  • 1 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce, for the patties
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 1/4 cup unsalted butter
  • 1/2 cup finely chopped mushrooms
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups beef broth
  • 1 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce, for the gravy
  • 1 Tbsp soy sauce
  • 4 cups cooked jasmine rice
  • 4 eggs, fried sunny side up
  • Chopped green onions, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Mix the ground beef, onion, garlic powder, egg, panko, Worcestershire sauce for the patties, and a good pinch of salt and pepper until combined. Shape into 4 patties about 1/2 inch thick.
  2. Cook the patties in a skillet over medium heat for 4 minutes per side, until done to your liking. Set them aside and wipe out the skillet.
  3. Melt the butter in the skillet over medium heat. Add the mushrooms and cook 5 minutes, until tender.
  4. Sprinkle the flour over the mushrooms and stir for 1 minute to make a blond roux.
  5. Slowly whisk in the beef broth, then the Worcestershire sauce for the gravy and the soy sauce. Simmer 5 minutes, stirring now and then, until thick. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. Divide the rice among 4 plates, top each with a patty, spoon on the gravy, and finish with a fried egg and green onions.

Notes: An 80/20 beef makes the juiciest patties. Fry the eggs while the gravy simmers so everything is hot at once.
