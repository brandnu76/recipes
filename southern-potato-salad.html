Easy Southern Potato Salad

Creamy, mustard-yellow Southern potato salad with russets mashed just a little, hard-boiled eggs, dill pickles, sweet onion, and Duke's mayo.

Adapted from gritsandpinecones.com

Ingredients Steps
10 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 10 servings

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 12 minutes

Total: 1 hour 27 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 3 lb russet potatoes, peeled and cubed (7 to 8 medium)
  • 2 tsp kosher salt, for the water
  • 4 hard-boiled eggs, peeled and chopped
  • 1/2 cup hamburger dill pickles, chopped
  • 2 Tbsp hamburger dill pickle juice
  • 1/2 cup Vidalia or sweet yellow onion, finely minced or grated
  • 1 cup mayonnaise (Duke's or Hellmann's)
  • 1/4 cup yellow mustard
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/4 tsp ground black pepper
  • Dash of paprika, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of water to a boil over high heat. Add the 2 tsp kosher salt and the potatoes and cook 10 minutes, until a piece mashes easily with a fork. Drain.
  2. While the potatoes cook, stir the mayonnaise, yellow mustard, onion, pickles, pickle juice, hard-boiled eggs, 1/2 tsp kosher salt, and black pepper together in a large bowl.
  3. Return the drained potatoes to the pot and press them with a masher 3 or 4 times, just to break up some pieces.
  4. Fold the potatoes into the dressing and dust with paprika.
  5. Refrigerate at least 1 hour and serve chilled.

Notes: The few presses with the masher are what make it creamy and help the potatoes soak up the dressing. Better made the day before.
