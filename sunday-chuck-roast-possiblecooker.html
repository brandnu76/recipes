Sunday Chuck Roast (PossibleCooker Pro)

Chuck roast braised in the PossibleCooker with onions, carrots, and potatoes.

IngredientsSteps
6 servings

Ingredients

  • Chuck roast, salt, pepper, oil, onions, garlic, tomato paste, wine, stock, thyme, bay leaves, potatoes, carrots, butter, and parsley

Instructions

  1. Season and sear the roast.
  2. Build the braising liquid and cook the roast until tender.
  3. Add vegetables, finish the braise, reduce the gravy, and serve.
