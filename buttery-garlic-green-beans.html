Buttery Garlic Green Beans

Fresh green beans simmered just tender, then tossed in a skillet with butter, garlic, and lemon pepper.

Adapted from Allrecipes

Ingredients Steps
4 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 4 servings

Prep: 5 minutes

Cook: 12 minutes

Total: 17 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 lb fresh green beans, trimmed and snapped in half
  • 3 Tbsp butter
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 pinches lemon pepper
  • Salt, to taste

Instructions

  1. Put the green beans in a large skillet, cover with water, and bring to a boil. Lower the heat to medium-low and simmer 5 minutes, until they start to soften. Drain the water.
  2. Add the butter to the green beans and stir until it melts, about 2 minutes.
  3. Add the garlic and cook, stirring, until tender and fragrant, about 3 minutes. Season with the lemon pepper and salt.

Notes: For bacon-y beans, cook 2 chopped slices of bacon in the skillet first and use the drippings with the butter.
