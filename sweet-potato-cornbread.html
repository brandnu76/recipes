Sweet Potato Cornbread

Moist, tender Southern cornbread with roasted sweet potato and brown butter, lightly sweet with a crisp golden top. Baked in an 8x8 metal pan.

Ingredients Steps
9 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 9 servings

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 30 minutes

Total: 1 hour 15 minutes (includes roasting the sweet potato)

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 large sweet potato, about 12 oz (1 cup mashed)
  • 1 cup stone-ground yellow cornmeal
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/3 cup brown sugar, packed
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 6 tablespoons butter, plus more for the pan
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3/4 cup buttermilk, full-fat
  • Honey, for serving

Instructions

  1. Roast the sweet potato whole at 400°F for 45–55 minutes, until a knife slides in with no resistance. Peel, mash smooth, and cool 10 minutes.
  2. Keep the oven at 400°F and butter an 8x8 metal baking pan.
  3. Brown the butter in a small pan over medium heat until it smells nutty and has brown flecks, about 4 minutes. Cool a few minutes.
  4. In a large bowl, whisk the cornmeal, flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon.
  5. In another bowl, whisk 1 cup mashed sweet potato, the brown sugar, eggs, buttermilk, and browned butter until smooth.
  6. Fold the wet into the dry just until no dry streaks remain; lumps are fine.
  7. Spread the batter in the pan and bake 25–30 minutes, until golden, the edges pull away, and a toothpick comes out clean (about 200°F in the center).
  8. Cool 10 minutes, then cut into 9 squares. Serve warm with butter and honey.

Notes: Serve warm with butter and honey; great with pulled pork, chili, or greens. Keeps 2 days wrapped at room temperature or 5 days refrigerated; rewarm at 350°F for 8 minutes (the microwave makes it gummy). Roasting rather than boiling concentrates the sugar and keeps the batter from going watery. A spoonful of bacon fat in the pan before baking is never wrong.
