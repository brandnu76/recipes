Cajun Chicken Pasta

Cajun-seasoned chicken thighs seared golden and tossed with penne or fettuccine, sweet peppers, onion, and a creamy parmesan Cajun sauce.

Adapted from Allrecipes

Ingredients Steps
8 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 8 servings

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 30 minutes

Total: 45 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, sliced into strips
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil, for the chicken
  • 2 Tbsp Cajun seasoning
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 lb fettuccine or penne pasta
  • 2 Tbsp butter
  • 1 Tbsp olive oil, for the sauce
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 bell peppers (red and yellow), sliced
  • 8 oz fresh mushrooms, sliced, optional
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 1 cup grated parmesan cheese, plus more for serving
  • 1 tsp Cajun seasoning, for the sauce
  • 1/2 tsp salt, for the sauce
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper, for the sauce
  • 1 Tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta until al dente, about 10 minutes. Scoop out 1/2 cup of the pasta water, then drain.
  2. While the water heats, toss the chicken with the 2 Tbsp Cajun seasoning, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and black pepper.
  3. Heat the olive oil for the chicken in a large deep skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the chicken 4 minutes per side, until golden and cooked through. Move it to a plate.
  4. In the same skillet, melt the butter with the olive oil for the sauce. Add the onion, garlic, bell peppers, and mushrooms and cook until softened, about 4 minutes.
  5. Pour in the chicken broth and heavy cream, then stir in the parmesan, the Cajun seasoning for the sauce, and the salt and pepper for the sauce. Simmer 5 minutes, stirring now and then, until slightly thickened.
  6. Add the pasta and chicken to the skillet and toss to coat, loosening with the reserved pasta water as needed.
  7. Top with the parsley and extra parmesan and serve.

Notes: Merged from two saved Cajun Chicken Pasta recipes: the fuller cream-and-broth version as the base, with the boneless thighs and optional mushrooms from the Allrecipes version. Thighs stay juicier than breast; breast strips work with the same timing. Cajun blends vary in salt, so taste before adding the extra salt.
