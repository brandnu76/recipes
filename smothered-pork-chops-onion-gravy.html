Smothered Pork Chops with Onion Gravy

Thick bone-in pork chops seared golden, then simmered under a lid in a deep HexClad pan with silky onion gravy made from the drippings and bone broth, spooned over white rice.

Ingredients Steps
4 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 4 servings

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 40 minutes

Total: 50 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 4 bone-in pork chops, 1 inch thick (about 2 1/2 lb)
  • 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 Tbsp bacon grease or butter
  • 2 Tbsp salted butter
  • 2 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 cups chicken bone broth
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 1/2 cups long-grain white rice
  • 3 cups water
  • 2 green onions, sliced

Instructions

  1. Rinse the rice and combine it with the water and 1/2 tsp of the salt in a medium saucepan. Bring to a boil, cover, and simmer on low 18 minutes, then rest off the heat, covered, until serving.
  2. While the rice cooks, season the pork chops with the rest of the salt, the pepper, garlic powder, and smoked paprika, then dredge them in the flour, saving 2 Tbsp of it.
  3. Heat the bacon grease in a deep HexClad pan with a lid over medium-high heat. Sear the pork chops until golden, 4 minutes per side, then move them to a plate.
  4. Lower the heat to medium, add the butter and yellow onions, and cook, stirring, until soft and browned, 8 minutes. Sprinkle in the saved flour and stir 1 minute.
  5. Whisk in the chicken bone broth, heavy cream, and thyme and bring to a simmer. Return the pork chops, cover, and simmer on low 15 minutes, until tender and 145°F.
  6. Serve the pork chops and onion gravy over the rice, topped with the green onions.

Notes: Bone-in rib or shoulder (blade) chops have the most fat and stay the juiciest when smothered. If the gravy gets too thick, loosen it with a splash of broth.
