Mushroom Rice Pilaf

Long-grain rice simmered with buttery sautéed mixed mushrooms, shallot, garlic, thyme, and a splash of dry sherry.

Adapted from aspicyperspective.com

Ingredients Steps
8 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 8 servings

Prep: 5 minutes

Cook: 45 minutes

Total: 50 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 lb mixed mushrooms, thinly sliced
  • 2 Tbsp butter
  • 1/4 cup chopped shallots
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 1/4 cups long grain rice
  • 2 1/2 cups vegetable broth (or water)
  • 1/3 cup dry sherry
  • 1 tsp salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 tsp ground black pepper

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter in a large lidded sauté pan over medium heat. Add the shallots, garlic, and thyme and cook until soft, about 3 minutes.
  2. Stir in the mushrooms and cook 10 minutes, until softened.
  3. Add the rice, broth, sherry, salt, and pepper. Cover and bring to a simmer.
  4. Lower the heat a little and cook 20 minutes, until the liquid is absorbed and small steam holes dot the top.
  5. Take the pan off the heat and let it rest, covered, 10 minutes. Fluff with a fork and season to taste.

Notes: Trim any tough stems before slicing. Chicken broth works in place of vegetable broth.
