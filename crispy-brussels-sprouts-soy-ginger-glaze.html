Crispy Brussels Sprouts with Soy-Ginger Glaze

Halved sprouts roasted dark and crisp on a hot sheet pan, then drizzled with a sticky make-ahead soy-ginger glaze and topped with crunchy fried onions, steakhouse style.

Adapted from copykat.com

Ingredients Steps
8 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 8 servings

Prep: 20 minutes

Cook: 40 minutes

Total: 1 hour

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

Soy-ginger glaze

  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 1/4 cups reduced-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 Tbsp minced fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar

Brussels sprouts

  • 2 lb Brussels sprouts, trimmed and halved
  • 1/4 cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt

Topping

  • 1/2 cup crispy fried onions

Instructions

  1. Glaze: In a small saucepan, stir the brown sugar, granulated sugar, and soy sauce together over medium heat. Simmer, stirring often, 12 minutes, until it has reduced by about a third.
  2. Stir in the ginger and rice vinegar, take the glaze off the heat, and let it cool completely, at least 1 hour; it thickens as it cools. It keeps a month in the fridge, so make it a day ahead.
  3. Heat the oven to 425°F. Trim the Brussels sprouts, pull off any yellow leaves, and halve them. Toss with the olive oil and kosher salt.
  4. Spread the sprouts on a rimmed sheet pan, cut side down, in one layer. Use two pans if they crowd; crowded sprouts steam instead of crisp.
  5. Roast 30 minutes, shaking the pan once halfway, until the cut sides are dark brown and the loose leaves are crackly.
  6. Pile the sprouts on a platter, drizzle with the soy-ginger glaze, and scatter the crispy fried onions over the top. Serve right away.

Notes: We cut the white sugar from 3/4 cup to 1/2 cup; the glaze still turns sticky and you only drizzle a little per plate. The recipe makes more glaze than you need, and the leftover is great on salmon, pork chops, or rice bowls. Pellet grill option: roast on a sheet pan at 425°F for the same time. Make the glaze ahead and Plan a Meal moves it into Do earlier. Adapted from the Red Lobster–style crispy Brussels sprouts at copykat.com.
