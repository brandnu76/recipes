Honey Roasted Carrots

Carrot sticks tossed with olive oil, honey, and thyme and roasted until the edges caramelize.

Ingredients Steps
8 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 8 servings

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 25 minutes

Total: 35 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 lb carrots, peeled and cut into sticks
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp honey or maple syrup
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp dried thyme, optional

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F.
  2. Toss the carrots with the olive oil, honey, salt, black pepper, and thyme and spread them in one layer on a sheet pan.
  3. Roast 20 minutes, stirring halfway, until tender and caramelized.
  4. Serve hot.

Notes: Thick carrots may need 25 minutes. Great with meatloaf and mashed potatoes.
