Lemon Herb Roasted Chicken Thighs with Garlic Butter Asparagus

Bone-in, skin-on thighs rubbed under the skin with lemon, garlic, and fresh herbs and roasted crisp, with parmesan garlic butter asparagus from the skillet.

Ingredients Steps
8 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 8 servings

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 40 minutes

Total: 50 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 8 large bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  • 3 Tbsp olive oil
  • 3 Tbsp butter
  • 6 cloves garlic, minced, divided
  • 2 lemons, zested and juiced, plus wedges for serving
  • 2 Tbsp each fresh rosemary, thyme, and parsley, chopped
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 2 lb asparagus, trimmed
  • 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese, freshly grated

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F and line a sheet pan with foil. Pat the chicken thighs dry and season them well with salt and pepper.
  2. Mix the olive oil, lemon zest, lemon juice, two-thirds of the garlic, and the herbs. Rub it under the skin and all over each thigh, and set them skin side up on the pan.
  3. Roast 35 minutes, until the skin is crisp and golden and the thighs reach 165°F.
  4. When the chicken is nearly done, melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the asparagus, season with salt and pepper, and cook 4 minutes, until crisp-tender.
  5. Add the rest of the garlic, toss 30 seconds, and take the skillet off the heat. Sprinkle with the Parmesan.
  6. Serve each thigh with asparagus, extra herbs, and a lemon wedge.

Notes: For extra-crisp skin, broil the thighs for the last few minutes. Thighs are even juicier taken to 175°F.
