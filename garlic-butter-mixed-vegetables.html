Garlic Butter Mixed Vegetables

A bag of frozen mixed vegetables simmered in broth, then tossed with garlic butter and parmesan. A ten-minute side.

Adapted from Giant Food Stores

Ingredients Steps
4 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 4 servings

Prep: 5 minutes

Cook: 10 minutes

Total: 15 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 (16-oz) bag frozen mixed vegetables
  • 1/2 cup vegetable broth
  • 1 Tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp minced garlic
  • 2 Tbsp grated Parmesan cheese
  • Pepper, to taste
  • 1 Tbsp chopped fresh parsley, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Combine the mixed vegetables and vegetable broth in a medium saucepan and cook over medium heat, stirring now and then, until heated through, about 7 minutes.
  2. Drain off the broth.
  3. Add the butter, garlic, and Parmesan to the pan, season with pepper, and stir until coated.
  4. Sprinkle with the parsley and serve.

Notes: Use 2 Tbsp butter for a richer side.
