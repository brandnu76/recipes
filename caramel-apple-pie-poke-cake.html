Caramel Apple Pie Poke Cake

A scratch buttermilk spice cake soaked with salted caramel and condensed milk, layered with cast-iron skillet apples, cinnamon cream-cheese whipped cream, caramel drizzle, and baked cinnamon-sugar pie crust crumbles.

Ingredients Steps
15 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 15 servings (one 9x13)

Prep: 45 minutes

Cook: 60 minutes

Total: about 4 hours 45 minutes (includes at least 3 hours chilling)

Difficulty: Intermediate

Ingredients

Spice cake

  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 1/2 cups sugar
  • 2 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp Diamond Crystal kosher salt
  • 2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp nutmeg
  • 1/4 tsp allspice
  • 3/4 cup (1 1/2 sticks) unsalted butter, melted and slightly cooled
  • 4 large eggs, room temperature
  • 1 cup buttermilk
  • 1/2 cup full-fat sour cream
  • 2 tsp vanilla

Skillet apples

  • 4 medium Granny Smith apples (about 2 lb), peeled and cut into 1/2-inch dice
  • 3 Tbsp butter
  • 1/3 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1 Tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tsp cornstarch mixed with 2 Tbsp water

Salted caramel

  • 1 cup sugar
  • 6 Tbsp butter, cubed
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream, warmed
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Soak

  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 3/4 cup of the salted caramel

Topping

  • 2 cups cold heavy cream
  • 4 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 1/2 cup powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 refrigerated pie crust
  • 1 Tbsp melted butter
  • 2 Tbsp sugar mixed with 1/2 tsp cinnamon

Instructions

  1. Caramel: Melt the sugar in a heavy saucepan over medium heat, stirring gently as it clumps, until it turns deep amber, 8 to 10 minutes. Whisk in the butter (it will foam hard), then slowly whisk in the warm cream. Boil 1 minute. Off heat, stir in salt and vanilla. Makes about 1 1/4 cups.
  2. Apples: Melt the butter in a 12-inch cast-iron skillet over medium heat. Add apples, brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt; cook 8 to 10 minutes until tender but holding shape. Stir in lemon juice and the cornstarch slurry; cook 1 minute until glossy. Spread on a plate to cool.
  3. Cake: Heat the oven to 350°F and butter a 9x13 pan. Whisk flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and spices. In another bowl whisk melted butter, eggs, buttermilk, sour cream, and vanilla. Fold wet into dry until just combined. Spread in the pan and bake 30 to 35 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
  4. Crunch: While the cake bakes, cut the pie crust into strips on a sheet pan, brush with melted butter, sprinkle with cinnamon sugar, and bake at 425°F for 8 to 10 minutes until golden. Cool and crumble.
  5. Poke and soak: Cool the cake 10 minutes. Poke holes every inch with the handle of a wooden spoon. Whisk the condensed milk with 3/4 cup caramel (rewarmed so it pours) and pour it slowly over the cake, working it into the holes. Cool 30 minutes.
  6. Layer: Spread the cooled apples over the cake. Refrigerate at least 2 hours or overnight.
  7. Top: Beat the cream cheese with powdered sugar, vanilla, and cinnamon until smooth. Add the cold cream and whip to stiff peaks. Spread over the apples.
  8. Serve: Just before slicing, drizzle with the remaining caramel and scatter the pie crust crumbles over the top.

Notes: Use a wooden spoon handle, not a fork; bigger holes mean visible caramel pockets. Cool the apples fully before layering or they will melt the whipped cream. Best made the night before. Leftovers keep 3 days covered in the fridge; store the crumbles separately.
