Cilantro Lime Rice

Toasted basmati rice cooked with garlic and lime zest, then tossed with fresh lime juice and lots of cilantro.

Adapted from Simply Recipes

Ingredients Steps
6 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 6 servings

Prep: 5 minutes

Cook: 35 minutes

Total: 40 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 Tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 1/2 cups basmati or long grain white rice
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 2 1/4 cups water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • Zest of 1 lime
  • 3 Tbsp lime juice
  • 1 cup lightly packed chopped cilantro, leaves and tender stems

Instructions

  1. Heat the olive oil in a medium saucepan over medium-high heat. Add the rice and stir to coat, then cook, stirring now and then, until it starts to brown, about 5 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic and cook 1 minute more.
  3. Add the water, salt, and lime zest and bring to a rolling boil. Cover, turn the heat to low, and cook undisturbed 15 minutes.
  4. Take the pan off the heat and let it sit, covered, 10 minutes. Fluff with a fork.
  5. Toss with the lime juice and cilantro and serve.

Notes: Check your rice package; some long-grain rices need a few more minutes. Great with fajitas, tacos, and grilled shrimp.
