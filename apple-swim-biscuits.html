Apple Swim Biscuits

Buttermilk swim biscuits with tender sautéed apple pieces folded in. The bottoms fry crisp in butter, and a cinnamon-sugar top and vanilla glaze finish them.

Ingredients Steps
12 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 12 servings

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 30 minutes

Total: 45 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups self-rising flour
  • 2 cups cold whole buttermilk
  • 1/2 cup (1 stick) plus 1 tablespoon salted butter, divided
  • 2 medium apples (Honeycrisp or Granny Smith), peeled and diced 1/2 inch (about 2 cups)
  • 2 tablespoons brown sugar
  • 3 tablespoons granulated sugar, divided
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons cinnamon, divided
  • 3/4 cup powdered sugar
  • 1-2 tablespoons milk
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 450°F.
  2. Cook the apples in 1 tbsp butter with the brown sugar and 1/2 tsp cinnamon for 4-5 minutes, until just tender. Cool slightly.
  3. Melt the stick of butter and pour it into an 8x8 or 9x9 metal pan.
  4. Whisk the flour, 2 tbsp sugar, and 1/2 tsp cinnamon. Stir in the buttermilk, then fold in the apples just until no dry flour remains.
  5. Spread the dough over the butter and score it into 9 or 12 squares all the way to the bottom.
  6. Mix 1 tbsp sugar with 1/2 tsp cinnamon and sprinkle it over the top.
  7. Bake 24-30 minutes, until the tops are deep golden and the centers spring back.
  8. Cool 10 minutes. Whisk the powdered sugar, milk, and vanilla, then drizzle over the top.

Notes: Best warm. Reheat 5 minutes at 350°F. Cook the apples first, because raw apples weep and make gummy pockets. A 10-inch cast iron skillet works too.
