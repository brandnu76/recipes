Sheet-Pan Salmon with Sweet Potatoes and Green Beans

Salmon with sweet potatoes and green beans finished with honey mustard.

IngredientsSteps
4 servings

Ingredients

  • Salmon, sweet potatoes, green beans, olive oil, Dijon mustard, honey, lemon, and garlic

Instructions

  1. Roast sweet potatoes until nearly tender.
  2. Add beans and salmon, brush with glaze, and roast until done.
  3. Serve with lemon wedges.
