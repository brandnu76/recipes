Cajun Sausage and Rice Skillet

Browned andouille, bell pepper, and toasted Cajun spices simmered with fire-roasted tomatoes, broth, and rice in one covered cast-iron skillet. Smoky, spicy, and done in under an hour.

Adapted from budgetbytes.com

Ingredients Steps
4 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 4 servings

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 35 minutes

Total: 50 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

Sausage and vegetables

  • 14 oz andouille sausage, sliced 1/4 inch thick
  • 1 Tbsp avocado oil
  • 1 bell pepper, diced
  • 1 small onion, diced
  • 1 stalk celery, diced

Spices

  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp dried thyme
  • 1/4 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/4 tsp onion powder
  • 1/8 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 1/8 tsp black pepper

Rice

  • 1 (15 oz) can fire-roasted diced tomatoes, with juices
  • 1 cup long-grain white rice
  • 1 1/2 cups chicken broth
  • 2 green onions, sliced

Instructions

  1. Set a deep 12-inch cast-iron skillet with a lid over medium heat with the avocado oil. Add the andouille sausage and brown it, stirring now and then, 8 minutes. The brown bits on the bottom are flavor for the rice.
  2. Add the bell pepper, onion, and celery and cook, stirring, 4 minutes, until they start to soften.
  3. Stir in the smoked paprika, oregano, thyme, garlic powder, onion powder, cayenne, and black pepper and cook 1 minute to toast the spices.
  4. Add the fire-roasted tomatoes with their juices, the rice, and the chicken broth. Stir and scrape up the browned bits from the bottom.
  5. Cover, raise the heat to medium-high, and bring to a full boil, about 4 minutes. Turn the heat to low and simmer, covered, 20 minutes without lifting the lid.
  6. Take the skillet off the heat and let it rest, covered, 5 minutes.
  7. Fluff the rice and fold the sausage through it. Top with the green onions and serve.

Notes: We added onion and celery to complete the Cajun trinity; it adds vegetables and flavor without changing the timing. If your cast iron has no lid, a sheet pan laid on top works. For more heat, double the cayenne or use hot andouille. Brown rice works too: use 2 cups broth and simmer 40 minutes. Adapted from the Cajun sausage and rice skillet at budgetbytes.com.
