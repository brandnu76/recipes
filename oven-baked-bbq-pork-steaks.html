Oven-Baked BBQ Pork Steaks

Rubbed pork shoulder steaks baked low and long until tender, then brushed and baked with barbecue sauce until sticky.

Adapted from justapinch.com

Ingredients Steps
5 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 5 servings

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 2 hours 5 minutes

Total: 2 hours 20 minutes

Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 1 package pork steaks (about 5)
  • 2 Tbsp dry bbq rub, plus more as needed
  • 1 Tbsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt and pepper
  • 1 bottle bbq sauce
  • Nonstick spray

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Line a rimmed sheet pan with foil and coat it with nonstick spray. Rub the bbq rub, garlic powder, and salt and pepper into both sides of each pork steak.
  2. Lay the steaks on the pan and bake 45 minutes.
  3. Turn the steaks and bake 45 minutes more.
  4. Spoon or baste off the extra grease. Brush the steaks with bbq sauce, turn them, and brush the other side.
  5. Bake 35 minutes more, until tender. Brush with more sauce and serve.

Notes: Pork steaks are cut from the shoulder, so they are well marbled and turn tender with the long bake. A turkey baster makes quick work of pulling off the grease.
