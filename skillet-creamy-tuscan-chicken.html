Skillet Creamy Tuscan Chicken

Seared chicken in a garlic cream sauce with sun-dried tomatoes and spinach.

IngredientsSteps
4 servings

Ingredients

  • Chicken, olive oil, garlic, sun-dried tomatoes, stock, cream, Parmesan, spinach, and butter

Instructions

  1. Season and sear chicken, then transfer it to a plate.
  2. Build the cream sauce with garlic and tomatoes.
  3. Wilt spinach, return chicken, and serve.
