The Best Coleslaw

Classic tangy coleslaw with two vinegars, a little sugar, and celery seed in a mayo dressing. Make it an hour ahead so the flavors blend.

Adapted from spendwithpennies.com

Ingredients Steps
6 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 6 servings

Prep: 15 minutes

Total: 1 hour 15 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 6 cups coleslaw mix (cabbage and carrots)
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise or salad dressing
  • 1 Tbsp white vinegar
  • 1/2 Tbsp cider vinegar
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 1/2 tsp celery seeds
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Whisk the mayonnaise, white vinegar, cider vinegar, sugar, celery seeds, salt, and pepper together in a large bowl.
  2. Add the coleslaw mix and toss to coat.
  3. Refrigerate at least 1 hour before serving so the flavors blend.

Notes: Keeps 2 to 3 days in the fridge; toss again before serving.
