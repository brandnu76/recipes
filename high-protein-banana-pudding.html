High-Protein Banana Pudding

A 10-minute banana pudding bowl: fat-free Greek yogurt, vanilla whey, sugar-free banana pudding mix, and zero-sugar Cool Whip, chilled in the freezer, then finished with whipped cream and crushed mini Nilla Wafers.

Ingredients Steps
1 serving

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 1 bowl

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 0 minutes

Chill: freezer until thickened

Total: about 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup fat-free Greek yogurt (225 g)
  • 1 scoop vanilla whey protein powder (30 g)
  • 2 tablespoons sugar-free banana pudding mix (15 g)
  • 2 tablespoons unsweetened almond milk (30 ml)
  • 1 tablespoon zero-calorie sweetener
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • Pinch of salt
  • 1/4 cup zero-sugar Cool Whip (20 g)
  • 2 tablespoons whipped cream, for topping (5 g)
  • 6 mini Nilla Wafers, crushed, for topping (12 g)

Instructions

  1. Add the Greek yogurt, vanilla whey, sugar-free banana pudding mix, almond milk, sweetener, vanilla extract, and a pinch of salt to a bowl. Mix until smooth and creamy.
  2. Add the zero-sugar Cool Whip and gently fold it in.
  3. Transfer to a serving bowl and freeze until chilled and thickened. Top with the whipped cream and crushed mini Nilla Wafers.

Notes

Pulled from Michael Kory (@michaelkory) on TikTok. Amounts, order, and macros are the ones in the caption. The clip is a paid partnership with Legion; the scoop in the video is Legion vanilla whey. Any vanilla whey at 30 g works.

The pudding mix is dry sugar-free instant banana pudding, 2 tablespoons (15 g), not a whole box. Do not cook it.

The caption does not give a freezer time. Check at 15 minutes. You want it cold and thicker, not a frozen block.

Creator numbers for this bowl: 390 calories, 48 g protein, 34 g carbs, 7 g fat. Brands move those numbers.
