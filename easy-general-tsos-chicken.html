Easy General Tso's Chicken

Cornstarch-dusted chicken thigh pieces pan-fried until golden, then tossed in a sticky hoisin, brown sugar, and garlic sauce. Takeout flavor in about 35 minutes.

Adapted from southernbite.com

Ingredients Steps
4 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 4 servings

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 20 minutes

Total: 35 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 1/2 cup cornstarch
  • 1/4 cup vegetable oil, plus more as needed
  • 1/2 cup hoisin sauce
  • 2 Tbsp soy sauce, plus more to taste
  • 1/4 cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 heaping tsp minced garlic (3 to 4 cloves)
  • 3 Tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1/2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper, optional
  • Sliced green onions and sesame seeds, for garnish

Instructions

  1. Put the cornstarch in a gallon zip-top bag, add the chicken, seal, and shake until every piece is coated.
  2. In a medium bowl, stir together the hoisin sauce, soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, rice vinegar, sesame oil, ground ginger, and crushed red pepper. Set the sauce aside.
  3. Heat the vegetable oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Shake the extra cornstarch off the chicken and fry it in batches without crowding, turning until browned on all sides and cooked through, about 8 minutes per batch. Add more oil between batches if the pan looks dry, and move the cooked chicken to a plate.
  4. Pour the sauce into the empty skillet, bring it to a boil, then lower to a simmer and cook 5 minutes, until thick and glossy.
  5. Return the chicken to the skillet and toss to coat. Taste for soy sauce and heat, then top with green onions and sesame seeds and serve right away.

Notes: Thighs stay juicy and forgiving here; breast works but dries out faster. Serve over rice. Add extra crushed red pepper to the sauce if you like it hot.
