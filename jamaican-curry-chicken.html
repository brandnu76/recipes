Jamaican Curry Chicken

Chicken thighs and Yukon gold potatoes simmered in a coconut milk curry with toasted curry powder, allspice, ginger, and jalapeño. Serve over rice with cilantro.

Adapted from wellplated.com

Ingredients Steps
4 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 4 servings

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 40 minutes

Total: 55 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 medium yellow onion, finely chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, very finely chopped
  • 2 jalapeño peppers, very finely chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp minced fresh ginger
  • 3 1/2 Tbsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp turmeric
  • 3/4 tsp allspice
  • 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper, plus more to taste
  • 2 medium Yukon gold potatoes, peeled and diced
  • 1 (15-oz) can light coconut milk
  • 1 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 1/2 tsp white wine vinegar
  • 1 tsp hot sauce, plus more to taste
  • Chopped fresh cilantro, for serving
  • Cooked rice, for serving

Instructions

  1. Sprinkle the chicken with the salt and set aside.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large heavy pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook, stirring now and then, until soft and translucent, about 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in the bell pepper, jalapeño peppers, garlic, and ginger and cook, stirring often, for 2 minutes.
  4. Add the curry powder, turmeric, allspice, and cayenne and stir constantly until the spices turn deep gold and fragrant, about 1 minute.
  5. Add the chicken and cook, stirring often, for 5 minutes, until golden outside.
  6. Add the potatoes and cook, stirring, for 3 minutes.
  7. Stir in the coconut milk, Worcestershire sauce, vinegar, and hot sauce and bring to a simmer. Lower the heat and simmer 20 minutes, stirring every few minutes, until the chicken and potatoes are tender and the sauce has reduced a little.
  8. Taste for salt and hot sauce, then serve over rice with plenty of cilantro.

Notes: AnyList showed this doubled; amounts are back at 1x. Full-fat coconut milk makes a richer sauce. Brown rice, quinoa, or cauliflower rice all work for serving.
