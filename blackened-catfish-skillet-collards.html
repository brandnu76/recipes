Blackened Catfish with Skillet Collards and Bacon

Catfish fillets crusted in homemade blackening spice and seared hot in a HexClad pan, with quick bacon-braised collard greens on the side.

Ingredients Steps
4 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 4 servings

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 25 minutes

Total: 40 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 4 catfish fillets (about 2 lb)
  • 4 slices thick-cut bacon, chopped
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 bunches collard greens (about 2 lb), stemmed and sliced into ribbons
  • 1 cup chicken bone broth
  • 1 Tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
  • 1 Tbsp smoked paprika
  • 2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp onion powder
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper
  • 4 Tbsp salted butter, melted
  • 1 lemon, cut into wedges

Instructions

  1. Cook the bacon in a deep HexClad pan over medium heat until crisp, 6 minutes. Add the onion and cook 3 minutes, then add the garlic for 1 minute.
  2. Add the collard greens a handful at a time, then the chicken bone broth and red pepper flakes. Cover and simmer over medium-low heat 15 minutes, until tender. Stir in the apple cider vinegar and keep warm.
  3. While the collards cook, mix the smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, thyme, oregano, salt, black pepper, and cayenne. Pat the catfish fillets dry, brush both sides with half the melted butter, and press on the spice mix.
  4. Turn on the vent fan and open a window. Heat a 12-inch HexClad pan over medium-high heat 2 minutes. Sear the catfish 3 minutes per side, drizzling the rest of the butter around it after you flip, until the fish flakes. Work in two batches if needed.
  5. Serve the catfish with the lemon wedges and the collards with their pot liquor.

Notes: HexClad is safe at medium-high, so you get a dark crust without the cast-iron smoke-out; do not crank it to high. Collards come in pre-cut bags too, which saves 10 minutes of stemming.
