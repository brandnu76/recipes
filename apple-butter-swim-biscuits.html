Apple Butter Swim Biscuits

Buttermilk drop biscuits baked in a pool of melted butter, so the bottoms fry crisp and golden while the tops stay soft. Apple butter is swirled through the batter, and a cinnamon glaze finishes them.

Ingredients Steps
12 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 12 servings

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 25 minutes

Total: 40 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups self-rising flour (White Lily if possible)
  • 2 cups cold whole buttermilk
  • 1/2 cup (1 stick) salted butter
  • 3/4 cup apple butter, divided
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon, divided
  • 3/4 cup powdered sugar
  • 1-2 tablespoons milk

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 450°F. Melt the butter and pour it into an 8x8 or 9x9 metal pan.
  2. Whisk the flour, sugar, and 1/2 tsp cinnamon. Stir in the buttermilk until just combined; the dough will be shaggy and wet.
  3. Spread the dough into the pan over the butter; butter will flood the top.
  4. Dollop 1/2 cup apple butter over the top and swirl it in with a knife.
  5. Score into 9 or 12 squares all the way to the bottom.
  6. Bake 22-28 minutes, until the tops are deep golden and the edges crisp.
  7. Cool 10 minutes. Whisk the powdered sugar, remaining cinnamon, and milk, then drizzle over the top. Serve the remaining apple butter on the side.

Notes: Best warm the same day. Reheat 5 minutes at 350°F. A 10-inch cast iron skillet works too.
