Grandmother's Cornbread

Jiffy mix made richer and sturdier with mayonnaise, eggs, oil, and a little sugar. A thick, moist pan of cornbread that holds up to a bowl of beans.

Adapted from Food.com

Ingredients Steps
6 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 6 servings

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 30 minutes

Total: 40 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 (8-oz) box Jiffy cornbread mix
  • 1/2 cup mayonnaise
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/4 cup oil
  • 2 Tbsp sugar

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease an 8 x 8-inch baking dish.
  2. Put the cornbread mix in a bowl, add the eggs and oil, then the mayonnaise and sugar.
  3. Stir well; the batter will be thick. Spread it in the baking dish.
  4. Bake 30 minutes, until golden and a toothpick comes out clean.

Notes: Some ovens take up to 45 minutes; start checking at 30. Bake it in a buttered cast-iron skillet for crispier edges. Made for Crockpot Pinto Beans.
