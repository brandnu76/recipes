Jo Mama's Big-Batch Italian Sausage Spaghetti

A big pot of Italian sausage tomato sauce, simmered low for an hour or more with basil, a touch of brown sugar, and a pinch of baking soda to smooth out the acidity.

Adapted from Food.com

Ingredients Steps
10 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 10 to 14 servings

Prep: 20 minutes

Cook: 1 hour 15 minutes

Total: 1 hour 35 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 2 lb Italian sausage (mild or hot), casings removed
  • 1 small onion, chopped, optional
  • 3 to 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 (28-oz) can diced tomatoes
  • 2 (6-oz) cans tomato paste
  • 2 (15-oz) cans tomato sauce
  • 2 cups water
  • 3 tsp dried basil
  • 2 tsp dried parsley flakes
  • 1 1/2 tsp brown sugar
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp crushed red pepper flakes
  • 1/4 tsp coarse ground black pepper
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 lb thin spaghetti
  • 1 spaghetti squash, cooked, optional
  • Parmesan cheese, for serving

Instructions

  1. Brown the sausage in a large heavy stockpot over medium heat, breaking it up as it cooks, about 10 minutes.
  2. Add the onion and cook, stirring now and then, until softened, about 5 minutes.
  3. Stir in the garlic, diced tomatoes, tomato paste, tomato sauce, and water, then the basil, parsley, brown sugar, salt, red pepper flakes, black pepper, and baking soda. It will foam a little from the baking soda.
  4. Bring just barely to a boil, then turn the heat to low and simmer 1 hour, stirring often so the bottom doesn't scorch.
  5. Cook the spaghetti according to the package, about 10 minutes, and drain.
  6. Spoon the sauce over the spaghetti (or spaghetti squash) and top with Parmesan.

Notes: AnyList showed this doubled; amounts are back at 1x, which still makes a big pot. A longer simmer only makes it better; add a splash of water if it gets thick. The sauce freezes well.
