Quick Broccoli and Cheese

Steamed broccoli under a fast, smooth cheddar and parmesan sauce made in the same skillet.

Adapted from spendwithpennies.com

Ingredients Steps
4 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 4 servings

Prep: 5 minutes

Cook: 10 minutes

Total: 15 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 4 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 Tbsp cornstarch
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • 1 Tbsp grated parmesan cheese
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Put the broccoli and water in a nonstick skillet and bring to a simmer over medium heat. Cover and steam 4 minutes, until bright green and tender. Move it to a bowl and cover to keep warm.
  2. Whisk the milk and cornstarch together, pour into the empty skillet, and whisk over medium heat until thickened, about 3 minutes.
  3. Turn the heat to low and whisk in the cheddar and parmesan until smooth.
  4. Season with salt and pepper and pour over the broccoli.

Notes: Sharp cheddar gives the boldest sauce.
