High-Protein Apple and Berry Crumble

Granny Smith apples and frozen mixed berries under an oat, egg, and protein crumble, baked 25 minutes, then served with high-protein vanilla yogurt.

Ingredients Steps
6 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 6 servings

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 25 minutes

Total: 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 3 medium Granny Smith apples (1 lb 2 oz), cored and chopped (about 4 cups)
  • 1 1/2 cups frozen mixed berries (7 oz)
  • 2 tablespoons plus 1 teaspoon sugar-free maple syrup
  • 3/4 teaspoon ground cinnamon, divided
  • 3 cups old-fashioned rolled oats
  • 2 scoops vanilla protein powder (about 2/3 cup)
  • Pinch of salt
  • 3 large eggs
  • 2 tablespoons PB2 powdered peanut butter
  • 2 cups high-protein vanilla yogurt, to serve

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F. Lightly grease an 8-inch square or 2-quart baking dish.
  2. Put the chopped Granny Smith apples and frozen berries in the dish. Add the sugar-free maple syrup and 1/2 teaspoon of the cinnamon. Toss.
  3. In a bowl, mix the oats, protein powder, remaining 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon, and a pinch of salt. Add the eggs and PB2. Stir until the oats are coated and the mix looks like a thick crumble.
  4. Spread the oat mix over the fruit and smooth the top.
  5. Bake 25 minutes, until the top is set and the fruit is bubbling at the edges.
  6. Serve warm with the vanilla yogurt, about 1/3 cup per serving.

Notes

US measures from the caption amounts: 500 g apples, 200 g frozen mixed berries, 35 ml sugar-free maple syrup, 2 g cinnamon, 250 g rolled oats, 60 g protein powder, 13 g PB2, 480 g vanilla yogurt. Oats use the USDA cup weight (81 g per cup). Protein powder volume varies by brand; 2 scoops at 30 g each is the reliable measure.

Pulled from Coen Sealey (@coensealey.fit) on TikTok, creator credit @gymtrav. The pinch of salt is spoken, not listed in the caption. Yogurt is not baked in. US swap for YoPRO: vanilla Greek yogurt or a high-protein cup (Oikos Pro, Ratio, Two Good).

Creator numbers per serve, yogurt included: 371 calories, 27.1 g protein, 44.5 g carbs, 6.5 g fat. Brands move those numbers.
