High-Protein Apple and Berry Crumble

Granny Smith apples and frozen mixed berries under an oat, egg, and protein crumble, baked 25 minutes, then served with high-protein vanilla yoghurt.

Ingredients Steps
6 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 6 servings

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 25 minutes

Total: 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 500 g Granny Smith apples (about 3 medium), cored and chopped
  • 200 g frozen mixed berries (about 1 1/2 cups)
  • 35 ml sugar-free maple syrup (2 tablespoons plus 1 teaspoon)
  • 2 g ground cinnamon (1 teaspoon), divided
  • 250 g old-fashioned rolled oats (about 2 3/4 cups)
  • 60 g vanilla protein powder (about 2 scoops)
  • Pinch of salt
  • 3 large eggs
  • 13 g PB2 powdered peanut butter (2 tablespoons)
  • 480 g high-protein vanilla yoghurt (about 2 cups), to serve

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 180°C (350°F). Lightly grease a medium baking dish, about 8-inch square or 2-quart.
  2. Put the chopped Granny Smith apples and frozen berries in the dish. Add the sugar-free maple syrup and half the cinnamon. Toss.
  3. In a bowl, mix the oats, protein powder, remaining cinnamon, and a pinch of salt. Add the eggs and PB2. Stir until the oats are coated and the mix looks like a thick crumble.
  4. Spread the oat mix over the fruit and smooth the top.
  5. Bake 25 minutes, until the top is set and the fruit is bubbling at the edges.
  6. Serve warm with the vanilla yoghurt, about 80 g (1/3 cup) per serving.

Notes

Pulled from Coen Sealey (@coensealey.fit) on TikTok, creator credit @gymtrav. Amounts and per-serve macros are the caption. The method is the voiceover: fruit base first, then the oat-egg-protein crumble, 25 minutes at 180°C, yoghurt on the side.

The pinch of salt is spoken, not listed in the caption. He names honey, then uses sugar-free maple to cut calories. Caption amount is 35 ml sugar-free maple. Cinnamon goes on the fruit and in the crumble; caption total is 2 g.

Yoghurt is not baked in. The 480 g YoPRO vanilla is the serve-with, and it is included in the creator macros. US swap: vanilla Greek yogurt or a high-protein cup (Oikos Pro, Ratio, Two Good). Emerald Labs in the clip is vanilla whey; any vanilla protein at 60 g works. PB2 is the same product.

Creator numbers per serve, yoghurt included: 371 calories, 27.1 g protein, 44.5 g carbs, 6.5 g fat. Brands move those numbers.
