Low-Country Boil

The classic coastal Georgia crowd pot: red potatoes, sweet onions, smoked sausage, corn, and shrimp boiled in seasoned water and poured out on the table.

Adapted from Food Network (Trisha Yearwood)

Ingredients Steps
8 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 8 to 10 servings

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 50 minutes

Total: 1 hour 5 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup concentrated Louisiana-style shrimp and crab boil seasoning (such as Zatarain's)
  • 4 lb medium red potatoes
  • 2 to 3 medium sweet onions, such as Vidalia, peeled and quartered if large
  • 2 1/2 lb smoked pork sausage links, cut into 3-inch pieces
  • 8 ears of corn, cut in half
  • 4 lb medium shrimp, shell on

Instructions

  1. Fill a 7-gallon stockpot halfway with water (or split everything between 2 large pots). Stir in the boil seasoning and bring to a rolling boil over high heat, about 15 minutes.
  2. Add the whole potatoes, let the water return to a boil, and cook 5 minutes.
  3. Add the onions and sausage, bring back to a boil, and cook 15 minutes.
  4. Add the corn, bring back to a boil, and cook 10 minutes, until the potatoes are tender.
  5. Add the shrimp, bring back to a boil, and cook until they turn pink, about 3 minutes.
  6. Drain everything through a colander and discard the liquid. Pour it out onto a newspaper-covered table or a big platter and serve.

Notes: Serve with melted butter, cocktail sauce, or the Seafood Boil Sauce. A fatty smoked kielbasa or andouille gives the pot the most flavor.
