Keto Salmon Cakes with Avocado Cream

Canned salmon cakes bound with almond flour and mayo, spiked with jalapeño and red onion, pan-fried crisp and served with a cool avocado-cilantro cream.

Adapted from heyketomama.com

Ingredients Steps
3 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 3 servings (6 small cakes)

Prep: 30 minutes

Cook: 10 minutes

Total: 40 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 (14.75-oz) can salmon, drained very well
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup almond flour, plus more if needed
  • 3 Tbsp mayo or spicy mayo
  • 3 Tbsp finely diced red onion
  • 1 small jalapeño, finely chopped
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp chili powder
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper
  • Salt, only if needed
  • 1 to 2 Tbsp avocado oil, for frying
  • 1 avocado, for the sauce
  • 1/3 cup sour cream
  • 1/4 cup cilantro
  • 1 Tbsp avocado oil, for the sauce
  • 1 to 2 tsp water, to thin
  • Juice of 1/4 lemon
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

Instructions

  1. Press as much liquid as you can out of the salmon; extra water keeps the cakes from crisping. Flake it into a bowl and pick out any skin or large bones you don't want.
  2. Add the eggs, almond flour, mayo, red onion, jalapeño, garlic powder, chili powder, and black pepper and mix gently. Let the mixture sit 10 minutes so the almond flour can soak up moisture.
  3. Squeeze a handful; it should hold together. If it's wet, add almond flour a tablespoon at a time; if dry, add mayo a teaspoon at a time. Shape into 6 cakes about 1/2 inch thick.
  4. Refrigerate the cakes 10 minutes to firm up.
  5. Heat the avocado oil for frying in a skillet over medium heat. Fry the cakes 4 minutes per side, flipping once, until golden and crisp. Leave them alone so the crust can form.
  6. While they cook, blend the avocado, sour cream, cilantro, avocado oil for the sauce, water, lemon juice, salt, and pepper until smooth. Serve the cakes hot with the avocado cream.

Notes: AnyList showed this one doubled; amounts here are back at 1x (one can of salmon, six cakes). Double everything for six servings.
