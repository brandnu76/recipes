Jamaican Oxtail Stew

Oxtails seasoned with Worcestershire, brown sugar, and allspice, browned and braised on the stovetop with carrots, peppers, thyme, and a whole scotch bonnet, then finished with butter beans.

Adapted from aspicyperspective.com

Ingredients Steps
6 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 6 servings

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 2 hours 45 minutes

Total: 3 hours

Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 3 lb oxtails
  • 3 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 3 Tbsp brown sugar
  • 1 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp allspice
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 large onion, peeled and chopped
  • 4 to 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • 3 to 4 large carrots, peeled and cut into 1-inch chunks
  • 2 bell peppers, seeded and cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 1 scotch bonnet pepper (or habanero), left whole
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 2 Tbsp fresh thyme leaves (or 2 tsp dried)
  • 2 Tbsp ketchup
  • 1 Tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 2 cups beef broth
  • 16 oz canned butter beans, drained
  • 1 Tbsp cornstarch
  • 1/2 cup chopped scallions

Instructions

  1. Put the oxtails in a large baking dish with the Worcestershire sauce, brown sugar, salt, black pepper, and allspice and toss to coat.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large 6- to 8-quart pot over medium heat. Brown half the oxtails 3 minutes per side, moving them around so the sugar doesn't burn. Repeat with the rest and set them aside.
  3. Add the onion and garlic to the pot and cook 2 minutes.
  4. Return the oxtails to the pot and top with the carrots, bell peppers, whole scotch bonnet, bay leaves, thyme, ketchup, and vinegar. Pour the beef broth over the top.
  5. Cover with a heavy lid and simmer on medium-low for 2 hours, until the meat is fork-tender.
  6. Toss the butter beans with the cornstarch, stir them into the stew, and simmer 5 minutes more.
  7. Sprinkle with the scallions and serve on its own or with rice.

Notes: Big, meaty oxtails can need a third hour; keep going until the meat pulls easily from the bone. Leave the scotch bonnet whole for flavor without too much heat, and fish it out before serving. Instant Pot: about 45 minutes at high pressure after browning. Slow cooker: low about 8 hours.
