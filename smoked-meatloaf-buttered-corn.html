Pellet-Grill Smoked Meatloaf with Buttered Corn

A juicy beef-and-pork meatloaf smoked low on the pellet grill, lacquered with a tangy brown sugar glaze, with skillet corn finished in plenty of butter.

Ingredients Steps
6 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 6 servings

Prep: 20 minutes

Cook: 2 hours 10 minutes

Total: 2 hours 40 minutes

Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 lb ground beef, 80/20
  • 1 lb ground pork
  • 2 large eggs
  • 3/4 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 1/2 cup whole milk
  • 1 medium onion, grated
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 Tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 cup ketchup
  • 1/3 cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 Tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 1 lb frozen sweet corn
  • 3 Tbsp salted butter
  • 1 Tbsp fresh parsley, chopped

Instructions

  1. Fill the pellet grill with hickory or cherry pellets and preheat it to 250°F, about 15 minutes.
  2. While it heats, stir the panko into the milk in a large bowl and let it soak 5 minutes. Add the eggs, onion, garlic, Worcestershire sauce, Dijon mustard, salt, pepper, and smoked paprika and mix well.
  3. Add the ground beef and ground pork and mix with your hands just until combined, 2 minutes. Shape it into a 9 by 5-inch loaf on a grill mat or a perforated pan.
  4. Smoke the meatloaf at 250°F for 90 minutes.
  5. Meanwhile, whisk the ketchup, brown sugar, and apple cider vinegar in a small bowl for the glaze.
  6. Brush half the glaze over the meatloaf, raise the grill to 325°F, and cook 30 minutes, until the center reads 160°F. Brush on the rest of the glaze for the last 10 minutes.
  7. Rest the meatloaf 10 minutes before slicing. While it rests, melt the butter in a HexClad skillet over medium heat, add the sweet corn, and cook, stirring, 8 minutes until hot and lightly browned. Season with salt and the parsley and serve with the meatloaf.

Notes: Grating the onion keeps the loaf tender with no crunchy bits. Leftover slices are great seared in butter for sandwiches the next day.
