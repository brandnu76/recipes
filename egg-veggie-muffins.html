Egg & Veggie Muffins

Fluffy baked egg cups loaded with sauteed peppers, mushrooms, spinach and sharp cheddar; a make-ahead brunch that reheats in under a minute.

Ingredients Steps
6 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 12 muffins (6 servings)

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 30 minutes

Total: 45 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 12 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup whole milk
  • 1 Tbsp butter, plus more for the pan
  • 1/2 medium yellow onion, diced
  • 1 red bell pepper, diced
  • 1 cup mushrooms, chopped
  • 1 cup baby spinach, chopped
  • 1 tsp kosher salt, divided
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1/4 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 cup sharp cheddar cheese, shredded
  • 2 green onions, sliced

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F and generously butter a 12-cup muffin tin, 10 minutes.
  2. Melt the butter in a HexClad skillet over medium heat. Add the onion, bell pepper, mushrooms and 1/4 tsp of the salt and cook until soft and the liquid has cooked off, 6 minutes. Stir in the spinach until wilted, 1 minute. Let cool 5 minutes.
  3. Whisk the eggs, milk, remaining 3/4 tsp salt, black pepper and garlic powder until smooth, 2 minutes.
  4. Divide the cooked vegetables, cheddar and green onions among the muffin cups, then pour in the egg mixture until each cup is about three-quarters full, 3 minutes.
  5. Bake until puffed and the centers are set (160°F), 22 minutes.
  6. Cool in the pan 5 minutes, run a thin knife around each muffin, and lift them out, 2 minutes.

Notes: Cooking the vegetables first keeps the muffins from turning watery. Refrigerate up to 4 days or freeze up to 2 months; reheat in the microwave 45 seconds (90 seconds from frozen). Swap in any leftover cooked vegetables or a different cheese.
