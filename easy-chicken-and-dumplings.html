Easy Chicken and Dumplings

A weeknight shortcut to the Southern classic: shredded chicken in a creamy broth with carrots and celery, and canned buttermilk biscuits rolled thin into tender dumpling strips.

Adapted from Pillsbury

Ingredients Steps
4 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 4 servings

Prep: 15 minutes

Cook: 35 minutes

Total: 50 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 (32-oz) carton reduced-sodium chicken broth (4 cups)
  • 3 cups shredded cooked chicken (about 3/4 lb)
  • 1 (10.75-oz) can condensed cream of chicken soup
  • 1/4 tsp poultry seasoning
  • 1 (16.3-oz) can Pillsbury Grands! Homestyle refrigerated buttermilk biscuits (8 biscuits)
  • 2 medium carrots, chopped (1/2 cup)
  • 3 celery ribs, chopped
  • Flour, for rolling

Instructions

  1. In a 4- to 5-quart heavy pot on the stovetop, bring the broth, chicken, soup, and poultry seasoning to a boil over medium-high heat. Lower the heat, cover, and simmer 5 minutes, stirring now and then.
  2. Meanwhile, on a lightly floured surface, roll or pat each biscuit 1/8 inch thick and cut into 1/2-inch strips.
  3. Turn the heat back to medium-high and bring the pot to a low boil. Drop in the biscuit strips one at a time, then add the carrots and celery.
  4. Lower the heat, cover, and simmer 15 minutes, stirring gently now and then so the dumplings don't stick, until they are cooked through.

Notes: AnyList showed this doubled; amounts are back at 1x. Rotisserie thigh meat gives the richest broth. Add a splash more broth if it gets too thick as it sits.
