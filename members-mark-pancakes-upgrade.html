Member's Mark Pancakes Upgrade

Turn Member's Mark buttermilk pancake mix into tender, diner-style pancakes with whole milk, an egg, melted butter, and vanilla.

Ingredients Steps
2 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 2 servings (about 6 pancakes)

Prep: 5 minutes

Cook: 15 minutes

Total: 20 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Member's Mark Buttermilk Pancake Mix
  • 3/4 cup whole milk (or 1/2 cup milk plus 1/4 cup buttermilk)
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 to 2 Tbsp melted butter, cooled slightly, plus more for the griddle
  • 1/2 to 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
  • 1 to 2 Tbsp club soda or seltzer, or a pinch of cinnamon, optional

Instructions

  1. Whisk the milk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla until combined.
  2. Add the pancake mix and stir gently with a fork just until the big dry pockets disappear. Lumps are good.
  3. Let the batter rest 5 minutes while you heat a griddle or heavy skillet over medium heat. A drop of water should sizzle and dance on it. Wipe it with butter.
  4. Pour 1/4-cup portions and cook until bubbles cover the top and the edges look set, about 2 minutes. Flip and cook until golden, about 1 minute more.

Notes: Start with the 3/4 cup milk and add a splash more for thinner pancakes. A splash of club soda right before cooking makes them extra light.
