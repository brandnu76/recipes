Lime and Fish Sauce Beef Salad

Seared strips of chuck roast tossed warm with napa cabbage, tomato, and cucumber in a bright lime and fish sauce dressing, served over white rice.

Ingredients Steps
6 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 6 servings

Prep: 20 minutes

Cook: 15 minutes

Total: 35 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 chuck roast (about 2 lb), sliced into thin strips
  • 2 Tbsp cooking oil
  • Salt and pepper, to taste
  • 2 heads napa cabbage, chopped
  • 3 tomatoes, diced
  • 3 cucumbers, peeled and diced
  • 6 limes, juiced
  • 3 Tbsp fish sauce, or to taste
  • 1 tsp red pepper flakes, or to taste
  • Cooked white rice, for serving

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a large frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the beef strips in batches, season with salt and pepper, and sear until browned, about 4 minutes per batch. Move the cooked beef to a large bowl.
  2. Add the cabbage, tomatoes, and cucumbers to the bowl with the beef.
  3. Whisk the lime juice with the fish sauce, red pepper flakes, and a pinch of salt. Pour it over the salad and toss.
  4. Serve warm over white rice.

Notes: The saved recipe listed no amounts for the beef, oil, or dressing, so these are starting points; begin with 3 Tbsp fish sauce and adjust. Partly freezing the roast for 30 minutes makes thin slicing much easier.
