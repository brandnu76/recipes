Sheet Pan Steak Fajitas

Sirloin strips, onion, and a rainbow of bell peppers tossed with taco seasoning and roasted hot on one pan, finished with roasted lime.

Adapted from feelgoodfoodie.net

Ingredients Steps
6 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 6 servings

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 12 minutes

Total: 22 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 lb sirloin or flank steak, sliced into 1/2-inch strips
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • 3 bell peppers, thinly sliced
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 2 Tbsp taco seasoning
  • 1 lime, cut into wedges
  • Warm tortillas, for serving
  • Salsa, for serving
  • Fresh cilantro leaves, for serving

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F and line a sheet pan with foil.
  2. Put the steak in the middle of the pan with the onion on one side and the bell peppers on the other. Drizzle with the olive oil, sprinkle with the taco seasoning, and toss with your hands to coat. Tuck the lime wedges onto the pan.
  3. Roast 10 minutes, until the vegetables soften and the steak is browned outside.
  4. Squeeze the roasted lime over everything and serve with warm tortillas, salsa, and cilantro.

Notes: Give it 12 minutes for steak cooked past medium. Flank or skirt steak sliced against the grain is the most tender.
