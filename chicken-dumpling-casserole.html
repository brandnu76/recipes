Chicken Dumpling Casserole

A layered Southern casserole: buttery shredded chicken, a Bisquick batter, and a creamy chicken stock pour that bakes into golden dumplings on top.

Adapted from justapinch.com

Ingredients Steps
8 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 8 servings

Prep: 30 minutes

Cook: 55 minutes

Total: 1 hour 25 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 3 boneless chicken thighs
  • 2 cups chicken stock (from cooking the chicken, or boxed)
  • 1 stick butter
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1 (10.5-oz) can cream of chicken soup (herbed adds more flavor)
  • 3 tsp chicken bouillon granules
  • 1/2 tsp dried sage
  • 1 tsp black pepper, plus salt as desired
  • 2 cups Bisquick
  • 1 (12-oz) bag frozen mixed vegetables, optional

Instructions

  1. Put the chicken in a saucepan with water to cover and bring to a boil. Turn off the heat, cover, and let it sit 15 minutes to finish cooking.
  2. Lift out the chicken, strain and save the cooking liquid for the stock, and shred the chicken once it's cool enough to handle. Heat the oven to 350°F.
  3. Melt the butter in a 9 x 13-inch or larger casserole dish in the oven. Spread the chicken over the butter and sprinkle with the black pepper and sage. For a pot-pie version, scatter the mixed vegetables over the chicken now. Do not stir the layers.
  4. Whisk the milk and Bisquick together and slowly pour it over the chicken.
  5. Whisk the chicken stock with the bouillon granules and soup until smooth, then slowly pour it over the Bisquick layer.
  6. Bake 50 minutes, until the top is golden brown.

Notes: Ovens and pan sizes vary; check at 50 minutes and give it up to 10 more if the top isn't golden. Swish a little warm stock in the soup can to get it all out. The original called for breasts in the cooking step and thighs in the list; thighs stay moister.
