Olive Garden-Style Zuppa Toscana

Spicy Italian sausage, tender potatoes, and kale in a creamy, buttery chicken broth, topped with bacon and parmesan. The copycat soup in about 40 minutes.

Adapted from lecremedelacrumb.com

Ingredients Steps
6 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 6 servings

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 30 minutes

Total: 40 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 lb spicy Italian ground sausage (mild for kids)
  • 4 Tbsp butter
  • 1/2 white onion, diced
  • 1 Tbsp minced garlic
  • 6 cups chicken broth
  • 2 cups water
  • 4 to 5 yellow potatoes, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 3 tsp salt, or to taste
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 2 cups heavy cream
  • 4 cups chopped kale
  • Chopped bacon and grated parmesan, for topping, optional

Instructions

  1. Brown the sausage in a large pot over medium heat, about 6 minutes. Lift it out with a slotted spoon and set aside.
  2. Melt the butter in the same pot and cook the onion until translucent, about 4 minutes. Add the garlic and cook 1 minute.
  3. Add the chicken broth, water, potatoes, salt, and pepper and bring to a boil. Boil 15 minutes, until the potatoes are tender.
  4. Stir in the kale, heavy cream, and sausage and simmer 3 minutes, until the kale wilts. Taste for salt and pepper.
  5. Serve topped with bacon and parmesan.

Notes: AnyList showed this doubled; amounts are back at 1x (it showed 1 onion at 2x, so 1/2 here). Start with 2 tsp salt if your broth is salty.
