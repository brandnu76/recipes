Tex-Mex Cheese Enchiladas with Chili Gravy

Old-school Tex-Mex: corn tortillas rolled around Colby-Jack and onion, smothered in a lard-based chili gravy, and baked until bubbly.

Adapted from homesicktexan.com

Ingredients Steps
4 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 4 servings

Prep: 20 minutes

Cook: 35 minutes

Total: 55 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup lard or vegetable oil
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 Tbsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1/2 tsp dried oregano
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 2 cups beef broth, chicken broth, or water
  • 2 Tbsp vegetable oil, for the tortillas
  • 8 corn tortillas
  • 3 cups shredded Colby-Jack cheese
  • 1 medium yellow onion, diced
  • Pickled jalapeños, for serving

Instructions

  1. Heat the lard in a pot over medium-high heat. Whisk in the flour and stir until lightly browned and fragrant, about 1 minute.
  2. Whisk in the chili powder, cumin, garlic powder, oregano, salt, and pepper, then the broth. Keep whisking until the gravy thickens, about 3 minutes.
  3. Turn the heat to low and simmer the gravy 15 minutes, thinning with more broth or water if it gets too thick. Meanwhile, heat the oven to 450°F.
  4. Pour the vegetable oil into a baking dish or large cast-iron skillet and turn the tortillas in it so they are coated. Warm them in the oven 3 minutes, until soft. Lift out the tortillas and cover them, then pour 1/2 cup of the gravy into the dish.
  5. Lay 1/4 cup of the cheese and 1 Tbsp of the onion down the center of each tortilla, roll it up, and set it seam side down in the dish.
  6. Pour the rest of the gravy over the enchiladas and top with the remaining cheese and onion. Bake 10 minutes, until bubbly and melted.
  7. Serve hot with pickled jalapeños.

Notes: Lard gives the gravy its classic flavor. Beef broth makes the richest gravy.
