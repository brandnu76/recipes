Keto Sheet Pan Burgers with Bacon & Jalapeño (Frozen Patties)

Quick keto sheet pan burgers made with frozen beef patties, bacon, caramelized onion, jalapeño rings, pepper jack cheese, and a spicy sweet mayo sauce. Ready in about 30 minutes.

Ingredients Steps Pellet grill
4 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 4 servings

Prep: 5 minutes

Cook: 22-27 minutes

Total: about 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 4 frozen beef hamburger patties (4–6 oz each preferred; 80/20 if available)
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt (or to taste)
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 12 slices raw bacon, cut in half
  • 8 slices of onion (about 1/4 inch thick)
  • 4 jalapeños, seeded and sliced into rings
  • 8 slices pepper jack cheese
  • 1/2 cup real mayonnaise (Hellmann’s or Duke’s preferred)
  • 2 tablespoons Sriracha hot sauce
  • 1 teaspoon Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 teaspoons granulated erythritol sweetener (such as Swerve)
  • Lettuce, tomatoes, and hamburger buns (or lettuce wraps for keto), for serving

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 425°F. Line a large baking sheet with foil for easier cleanup (optional but recommended). Place a wire rack on the sheet if available for better browning and less steaming.
  2. Place the frozen beef patties on the baking sheet. Season the tops evenly with the kosher salt, black pepper, and garlic powder.
  3. Arrange the bacon pieces, onion slices, and jalapeño rings around and between the patties on the same baking sheet.
  4. Bake for 20–25 minutes, until the patties reach an internal temperature of 160°F (use a meat thermometer). Flip the patties halfway through if not using a rack. Bacon and vegetables should be nicely cooked; add a few minutes if needed.
  5. Meanwhile, combine the mayonnaise, Sriracha, Worcestershire sauce, and erythritol in a small bowl. Stir until smooth. Refrigerate until ready to serve.
  6. Remove the sheet pan from the oven. Place 1–2 slices of pepper jack cheese on each burger patty (depending on size).
  7. Switch the oven to broil on high. Return the pan to the oven and broil for 1–2 minutes, until the cheese is melted and lightly browned. Watch closely to avoid burning.
  8. Remove from the oven. Build each burger with 1 patty, bacon, onion slice, jalapeños, and a generous drizzle of the sauce. Add lettuce, tomato, or other toppings as desired. Serve warm.

Notes: Frozen patties cook longer than fresh homemade ones. Standard 4 oz frozen patties work well; for larger portions closer to the original recipe, use thicker or multiple patties per serving. Internal temperature of 160°F ensures safety. Nutrition will vary with patty size—original fresh version was roughly 600 calories per large burger.

Pellet grill variation

Same burgers with a layer of real wood smoke, then a hard sear for the crust.

  1. Fire the pellet grill to 225°F with a bold wood like hickory or a competition blend. Mix the spicy mayo sauce first and chill it.
  2. Bacon first: lay the bacon on a sheet pan or rack and cook it on the grill at 400°F for 15–20 minutes until crisp, or cook it ahead and keep it warm. Drop the grill back to 225°F.
  3. Frozen patties: season with the salt, pepper, and garlic powder and smoke straight from frozen at 225°F for 30–40 minutes. This is where the smoke flavor comes in. Lay the onion slices and jalapeño rings on the grates or a grill pan alongside them to pick up smoke too.
  4. Fresh patties (if you're using them instead): season the same way and smoke at 225°F for 25–30 minutes, until about 130°F inside.
  5. Raise the grill to 400–450°F, or use searing grates or a sear zone. Sear the patties 2–4 minutes per side until they're crusted and hit 160°F internal. Char the onions and jalapeños for a minute or two while you're at it.
  6. Lay 1–2 slices of pepper jack on each patty for the last 1–2 minutes with the lid closed to melt.
  7. Rest the burgers 5 minutes, then build them with bacon, onion, jalapeños, and a heavy drizzle of the sauce over lettuce wraps or buns.
