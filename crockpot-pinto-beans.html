Crockpot Pinto Beans

Dry pinto beans slow-cooked all day with bacon fat, salt, pepper, and a bay leaf into a creamy, savory pot of beans.

Adapted from eatwellspendsmart.com

Ingredients Steps
6 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 6 servings

Prep: 10 minutes

Cook: 8 hours

Total: 8 hours 10 minutes

Difficulty: Medium

Ingredients

  • 1 lb dry pinto beans
  • 6 cups water (7 cups if you skip the soak)
  • 1 1/2 tsp sea salt
  • 1/2 tsp pepper
  • 1 Tbsp bacon fat (or a ham hock, or 2 slices chopped raw bacon)
  • 1 bay leaf, optional

Instructions

  1. Rinse and sort the beans, picking out any stones or shriveled beans. If you soaked them overnight, drain them.
  2. Put the beans in the slow cooker with the water, sea salt, pepper, bacon fat, and bay leaf.
  3. Cover and cook on low for 8 hours, until tender.
  4. Taste, add more salt if needed, and serve.

Notes: Soaking is optional: cover the beans with 2 inches of warm water and soak overnight, then use 6 cups fresh water. Unsoaked beans need the extra cup. High setting takes about 5 hours. Older beans can take longer; keep cooking until creamy. A ham hock makes the richest pot. Serve with Grandmother's Cornbread.
