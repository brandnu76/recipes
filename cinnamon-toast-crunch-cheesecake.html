Cinnamon Toast Crunch Cheesecake

Baked cheesecake with a cinnamon cereal crust and creamy vanilla filling.

IngredientsSteps
8 servings

Ingredients

  • Cinnamon Toast Crunch cereal, butter, cream cheese, sugar, eggs, vanilla, sour cream, and heavy cream

Instructions

  1. Prepare the cereal crust and bake until set.
  2. Beat the filling smooth, pour over the crust, and bake until the center gently jiggles.
  3. Cool completely, chill, and finish with whipped cream and cereal crumbs.
