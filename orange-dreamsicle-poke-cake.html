Orange Dreamsicle Poke Cake

Soft yellow cake soaked with orange gelatin, then topped with fluffy vanilla pudding–whipped topping. It tastes like a creamsicle melted into a sheet cake.

Ingredients Steps
12 servings

By Brandon Carroll

Yield: 12 servings

Prep: 20 minutes

Cook: 30 minutes

Total: 4 hours (includes chill)

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

  • 1 box yellow cake mix, plus eggs, oil, and water as the box directs
  • 1 (3 oz) box orange gelatin
  • 1 cup boiling water
  • 1/2 cup cold water
  • 1 (3.4 oz) box instant vanilla pudding mix
  • 1 cup cold whole milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 8 oz frozen whipped topping, thawed
  • 1 teaspoon orange zest, optional

Instructions

  1. Bake the cake in a greased 9x13 pan as the box directs. Cool 15 minutes, until warm but not hot.
  2. Poke holes all over with the handle of a wooden spoon, about 1 inch apart, going most of the way to the bottom.
  3. Stir the gelatin into 1 cup boiling water until dissolved, then stir in 1/2 cup cold water. Slowly pour over the cake, aiming for the holes. Chill 1 hour.
  4. Whisk the pudding mix, cold milk, and vanilla for 2 minutes until thick. Fold in the whipped topping and orange zest if using.
  5. Spread the topping over the chilled cake. Cover and chill at least 2 more hours, or overnight.

Notes: Pour the gelatin slowly so it sinks. Poke while the cake is still a little warm so the holes stay open. Keeps covered in the fridge 3–4 days. Don't freeze; the topping weeps.
